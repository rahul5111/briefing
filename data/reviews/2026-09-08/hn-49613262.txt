The work resolves the problem by establishing statements C and D in the official formulation, proving that the dynamics of the Navier–Stokes equations for fluid motion can develop a singularity in finite time. The solution describes a vortex that spirals inward and elongates while its energy remains finite, marking a breakdown in how the equations model continuous fluid motion. The effort began on Tuesday, September 1, after researchers heard rumors regarding Levent Alpöge and Tristan Buckmaster, and the agents arrived at their resolution on Saturday, September 5, about 88 hours after launch, with Lean formalization and verification taking an additional 17 hours via GPT-6 Astra. Across the Navier–Stokes effort, the agents sent 2.7 million messages and used approximately 130 billion output tokens. OpenAI stated that it does not intend to claim the Millennium Prize for the result, which was released to inform the world about the pace of AI progress and what to expect from upcoming models.
