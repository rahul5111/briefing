═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

On Tuesday, September 8, the United States struck multiple Iranian oil tankers following attempted missile attacks that targeted a US Navy warship. The strikes were reported by The Associated Press, citing an anonymous US official. Iranian media separately reported that a US projectile struck a small tanker about four miles from Kharg Island, a key oil export hub, with no casualties reported. Blasts were also heard on Kharg Island and in the southern port of Jask. 

Ahead of the strikes, Iran's Revolutionary Guard Navy warned tanker crews near Kuwait and Bahrain to immediately leave their vessels, stating the ships would be targeted. Iranian Armed Forces chief of staff Ali Abdollahi warned that Tehran would target US military bases across the region in retaliation. Additionally, the Revolutionary Guard claimed it intercepted and destroyed an MQ-1 drone over the Strait of Hormuz using an advanced defence system. 

The military escalation drove international benchmark Brent crude to briefly reach $99.46 a barrel on Tuesday. In tandem with the strikes, the US Treasury announced sanctions against 36 entities, targeting all remaining Iranian airlines not previously penalized, accusing the aviation sector of transporting weapons and illicit cargo. 

Despite the confrontation, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi stated that talks with Oman on managing the Strait of Hormuz had achieved significant progress. Meanwhile, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio accused Iran of orchestrating recent attacks by Houthi rebels against Saudi Arabia. In Lebanon, Israeli airstrikes in the south on Monday killed 13 people, according to the Lebanese Health Ministry, marking an escalation after weeks of calm.
