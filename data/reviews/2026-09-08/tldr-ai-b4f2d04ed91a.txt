═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

AI lab Anthropic has signed approximately $517 billion in compute capacity leases over the past 11 months, according to an analysis by The Information compiling public announcements and reporting. Since October of last year, the company has committed to 14.8 gigawatts of compute capacity to be accessed over the next few years. An unnamed source noted that Anthropic had previously secured between 1 gigawatt and 2 gigawatts of capacity prior to that timeframe. The largest shares of this new capacity will be provided by Google and Amazon Web Services, which will combined contribute 11 gigawatts. Anthropic's rapid scaling includes a $45 billion compute capacity agreement with Nscale signed in late August, a 191-megawatt agreement with Riot Platforms for a Texas data center, and a $200 billion agreement with Google for deployable TPUs. Beyond data center leases, Anthropic has secured major cloud capacity deals with Akamai, Amazon Web Services including a cluster of custom Trainium hardware, CoreWeave, and a $50 billion partnership with Fluidstack. Additional agreements involve SpaceX and xAI, Google Cloud, Lambda, Volta Infra, AMD, and Microsoft. Anthropic previously informed investors it expected to spend about $180 billion on renting servers through 2029, though this figure has substantially increased under the $517 billion total, which does not account for any potential unpublicized deals. Separately, Anthropic confidentially filed for an Initial Public Offering with the US Securities and Exchange Commission in June of this year, though no further updates have been released regarding the filing.
