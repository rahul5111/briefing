The Internet Archive is launching a matching gift campaign this September to support the infrastructure required to preserve and provide public access to 210 petabytes of digital knowledge. Under the initiative, monthly donations will be tripled by matching support, turning a twenty-five dollar monthly gift into seventy-five dollars, a fifty-dollar monthly gift into one hundred fifty dollars, and a one-hundred-dollar monthly gift into three hundred dollars. The digital library operates completely free of charge without selling user data or running ads, relying instead on donations that average about twenty-five dollars to build, maintain, and power its own servers, storage, and cooling systems. Universal access to all human knowledge is within our grasp. Our job is to put the best our world has to offer within the reach of our children, according to Brewster Kahle, Internet Archive Digital Librarian.
