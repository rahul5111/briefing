Jaden Caradine serves as a Pathways intern at NASA Langley Research Center in Hampton, Virginia, following five years as a United States Marine Corps mechanic, four of them stationed in Japan. Originally from the Salt Lake City area, Caradine later enrolled at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University's Daytona Beach campus to study aerospace engineering. At NASA Langley, he joined the Systems Analysis and Concepts Directorate, working specifically with the in-space servicing, assembly, and manufacturing team. During his time there, he helped curate the State of Play document, a resource consolidating ISAM sector activities across government, academia, and industry, and built an automated tool that scrapes aerospace news to streamline team updates. Dale Arney, an aerospace engineer and mentor to Caradine, noted that Jaden joined the team and immediately contributed to the update while creating a tool to speed up future reports. Reflecting on his choice to work at the agency, Caradine stated, NASA doesn't work for profit, we're here to remove barriers so that industry can eventually do the things they weren't able to do before.
