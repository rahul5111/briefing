The new model delivers state-of-the-art performance in computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work. OpenAI products currently reach more than one billion weekly active users and 2.5 million businesses across platforms including ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, Codex, and API applications. On the technical side, an earlier update using GPT-5.6 Sol reduced end-to-end serving costs by 20 percent and increased token-generation efficiency by more than 15 percent. Additionally, OpenAI announced Jalapeño, its first custom inference chip. In InferenceX tests across three public models using rated chip power to normalize comparisons, Jalapeño delivered 1.5 to 1.9 times as much peak token throughput per watt as the commercial systems tested, with end-to-end latency 1.7 to 3.6 times lower. OpenAI plans to begin deploying the custom chip by the end of the year alongside accelerators from NVIDIA, AMD, and other partners. Within OpenAI's own research organization, teams now utilize 3.1 agent-workdays of effort for every workday of human labor to handle tasks like resolving infrastructure problems and contributing code.
