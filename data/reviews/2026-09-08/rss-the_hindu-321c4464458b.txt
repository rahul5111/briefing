═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Mysuru City Corporation cleared nearly 1,000 metric tonnes of solid waste across 80 garbage-vulnerable spots during a campaign held from July 28 to August 31, 2026. The 222-hour initiative involved about 4,000 people and aimed to strengthen Mysuru's position in national cleanliness rankings while marking the 80th Independence Day celebrations. MCC Commissioner M.K. Savita led the effort with municipal officials, staff, and local residents. As MCC Commissioner M.K. Savita stated, We launched this initiative with the idea that all of us should work together to clean the city. Instead of leaving the task only to civic workers, we officials too can step in and clean the garbage. We did that, and the message went out to society, resulting in residents joining and supporting the initiative. This also encouraged and motivated our civic workers. Outstanding contributions from zone teams and individual pourakarmikas were recognized during a celebration event at the MCC on Tuesday, where the Gokulam crematorium GVP won first prize in the officials category and the Zone-7 C.V. Road team received the rolling trophy. The MCC plans to expand the phased cleanup program to eliminate all garbage-vulnerable points across Mysuru, with the long-term objective of making it the cleanest city in the country.
