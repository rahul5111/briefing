═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A new technical overview details how vLLM supports speculative decoding on AMD GPUs using the ROCm open software platform, specifically tested on AMD Instinct MI300X and MI355X accelerators. Speculative decoding speeds up large language model serving by pairing a lightweight draft component with a target model. The draft component proposes candidate future tokens, and the target model verifies them in a single forward pass, committing multiple output tokens when accepted while preserving the target model's behavior. The briefing outlines five specific drafting approaches: native Multi-Token Prediction modules, Gemma 4 MTP, EAGLE-3, DFlash, and DSpark. These methods differ in how the draft component receives target-model information and whether candidate tokens are generated sequentially, autoregressively, in parallel, or through hybrid approaches.
