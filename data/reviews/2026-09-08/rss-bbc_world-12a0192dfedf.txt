═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The UK has announced a comprehensive sanctions regime targeting illegal Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, banning settlement goods, advertising, and services linked to settlement expansion. Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband told MPs that the action, launched alongside France and Canada, marks the beginning of a new approach to the conflict, accusing the Israeli government of turning a blind eye to the ethnic cleansing of Palestinians by settlers. Miliband stated that the UK views the occupation as unlawful, citing a 2024 advisory opinion from the International Court of Justice, and announced that the new sanctions will be introduced over the next six to nine months. The decision follows Israel's opening of tenders to build approximately 1,200 settlement homes in the E1 area east of Jerusalem. Israel's Foreign Minister Gideon Saar condemned the move as an outrageous lie and announced immediate counter-measures, including shutting the UK consulate in East Jerusalem, banning 11 British MPs from entering Israel, and terminating UK training of Palestinian Authority forces. Saar stated that the hostile Labour government left Israel no choice but to respond, while President Isaac Herzog warned the decision would fall on the wrong side of history. The UK also issued a joint statement with 11 other nations pledging support for the two-state solution and restricting trade with settlements. Domestically, Chief Rabbi Sir Ephraim Mirvis called it a dark day for British Jews, while the Palestinian presidency welcomed the decision as an important step against Israel's colonial system.
