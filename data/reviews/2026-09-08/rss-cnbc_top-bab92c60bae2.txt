═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Qualcomm shares rose four percent on Tuesday following the announcement of a data center infrastructure partnership with Amazon Web Services. As part of the agreement, Qualcomm issued Amazon warrants to acquire 25 million shares at $161.26 each, representing a total investment of $4 billion. The warrants expire on September 3, 2036, and vest in tranches tied to commercial arrangement executions and the purchase of up to $60 billion in Qualcomm server chips and other technology. Qualcomm is collaborating with Amazon across multiple generations of customized silicon to build out AWS AI infrastructure with a specific focus on inference. According to a company statement, the collaboration brings together Amazon infrastructure with Qualcomm technology leadership in power-efficient processing, silicon design, and system-level integration as AI workloads grow exponentially. The partnership marks a major win for Qualcomm as it attempts to challenge Nvidia in the artificial intelligence market by targeting the growing demand for data center central processing units.
