OpenAI is preparing to introduce Managed Agents at its upcoming DevDay event, scheduled for September 29, 2026, at Fort Mason in San Francisco. The implementation broadly follows Anthropic’s managed agents offering, targeting both developers and business customers with capabilities for creating and managing different agents and environments while enabling specific skills and plugins. Codebase indications show a focus on hosted, long-horizon functionality that succeeds OpenAI's earlier custom GPTs introduced in November 2023 and the Agent Builder launched in October 2025, which is set to be wound down after November 30 alongside Evals. 

Alongside the developer tools, OpenAI is working on functionality that integrates its existing ChatGPT Ads and Ads Manager with agents, allowing ads to resolve directly to an agent that takes the lead and guides users through conversion funnels. This approach aims to optimize and customize funnels far better than traditional advertising solutions. Additional announcements expected at the livestreamed event include model updates and security solutions.
