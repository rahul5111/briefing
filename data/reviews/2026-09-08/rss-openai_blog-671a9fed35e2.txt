OpenAI is launching a multi-faceted initiative to support the journalism ecosystem, beginning with the 2026-2027 academic year at CUNY’s Newmark J-School and Northwestern University’s Medill School of Journalism. OpenAI is providing more than 400 ChatGPT Edu subscriptions to interested graduate students and faculty at those schools, collaborating specifically with the Tow-Knight Center for Journalism Futures at Newmark and Medill’s Knight Lab. 

As stated by Rich Gordon of Northwestern, the support ensures that equity of access is not a barrier, with Gordon noting that the Knight Lab has been working at the intersection of media and AI for more than a decade. Jarvis Watson of the Newmark J-School added that generative AI is a transformative technology that the industry must adapt to and shape, stating, We intend for the Newmark community to be at the forefront of this transformation.

This university program expands on OpenAI’s existing journalism efforts. These include a renewed partnership with the American Journalism Project giving more than 50 portfolio news organizations access to ChatGPT enterprise and API credits, continued support for the Lenfest Institute for Journalism’s AI Collaborative and Fellowship program, a 2024 partnership with WAN-IFRA supporting the Newsroom AI Catalyst accelerator for over 165 newsrooms, and ongoing work with INMA. Additionally, OpenAI provided a 2023 grant to launch the Ethics and Journalism Initiative at NYU’s Arthur L. Carter Journalism Institute and has hosted trainings this year at conferences for the National Association of Black Journalists, National Association of Hispanic Journalists, Asian American Journalists Association, and NLGJA: The Association of LGBTQ+ Journalists.
