═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Erling Haaland scored twice to give Manchester City a 2-0 away victory over Porto in their opening match of the Champions League. The win gives manager Enzo Maresca a perfect start with four wins from four games since succeeding Pep Guardiola, coming ahead of an upcoming matchday two fixture against Paris Saint-Germain and a Sunday derby against Manchester United. 

After a scoreless first half that saw Porto limit City's penetration, the visitors broke through immediately after the interval. Rayan Cherki drove into the area and fed Enzo Fernández, whose cross was headed into the turf and home by Haaland. With Porto pushing for an equalizer, Haaland secured the result late in the match with a right-foot stab. 

The victory is significant for City, who had lost six of their past nine away games in the competition and lost the 2021 Champions League final at the same venue. The three points provide a crucial fillip toward the accepted target of 16 points needed to qualify automatically for the knockout stage, particularly with a difficult home match against the double European champions looming next month. Maresca's side managed the win despite missing a rest for Haaland and handing a full debut to Ayyoub Bouaddi in a midfield alongside Fernández, while utilizing substitutions including Elliot Anderson and Iliman Ndiaye in the second half to help maintain control against Francesco Farioli's side.
