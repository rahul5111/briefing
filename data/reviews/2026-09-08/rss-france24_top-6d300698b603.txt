Jose Mourinho began his Champions League campaign with Real Madrid by defeating his former side Inter Milan 2-1 at the Santiago Bernabeu on Tuesday. The record 15-time European champions capitalized on two first-half defensive errors to take a two-goal lead. In the 14th minute, a poor pass from Curtis Jones placed Yann Bisseck under pressure, allowing Brahim Diaz to poke the ball through to Kylian Mbappe, who fired home the opening goal. Real Madrid added a second when Inter goalkeeper Josep Martinez dallied on the ball, enabling Fede Valverde to pry it away and score. Cristian Chivu's Inter side responded in the second half when Carlos Augusto smashed home a shot past Thibaut Courtois from Lautaro Martinez's cross in the 77th minute. Despite Inter's energetic attacks and subsequent chances from Alessandro Bastoni and Henrikh Mkhitaryan, Courtois made crucial saves to deny Marcus Thuram, Lautaro Martinez, and Bastoni. Inter goalkeeper Martinez also recovered from his earlier error to make sensational stops against Mbappe and Jude Bellingham to keep the visitors in the match. The victory gets Mourinho off to a triumphant start in the league phase following his return to the club, bouncing back from a recent La Liga defeat at Real Betis.
