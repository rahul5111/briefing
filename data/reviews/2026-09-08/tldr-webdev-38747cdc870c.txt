═══ 00_KEY_POINTS ═══

1. The greatest cost of artificial intelligence is the erosion of trust among open software practitioners.
2. Makers of open source software now face suspicion and anger regarding whether artificial intelligence was used.
3. Developers reluctantly adopt artificial intelligence because the industry has decided that this is how work is done.
4. Software developers have limited power to protect themselves when the jobs market is shrinking.
5. Practicing open software now requires great caution because developers face shunning or termination over artificial intelligence views.

═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Creating open source software has transformed from a connective, community-driven act into a practice fraught with suspicion and hostility due to the integration of artificial intelligence and corporate-mandated development tools. Software developers increasingly face intense scrutiny from peers who demand to know whether free software was built using machinery like AI, sometimes resulting in aggressive attacks against givers. This dynamic is fracturing the software developer community and upending its gift economy into what is described as a cold civil war beset with spy games. Developers are trapped between economic realities that strip away their autonomy, forcing them to adopt industry-standard tools they might otherwise reject, and a community culture where admitting to AI use can lead to social shunning, while speaking against it can result in termination by employers. This erosion of trust is causing cherished peers to leave the technology sector entirely to preserve their sanity.
