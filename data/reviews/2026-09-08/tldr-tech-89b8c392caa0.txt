═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI is currently testing a new feature that will allow ChatGPT to learn a user's writing style by connecting to third-party services like Gmail, Slack, Notion, and Google Drive. The upcoming tool was first spotted on X by Gael Breton and subsequently reported by Bleeping Computer. Screenshots from Breton show a description stating that ChatGPT will write in the user's voice by referencing examples from connected apps. For users who currently have access, the option appears in the settings menu under Personalization and Writing, where a toggle reads Reference my writing style alongside a prompt to set up the feature. While OpenAI has not yet made an official announcement, the company frequently tests new ideas with a select group of users before a wider rollout. One user who replied to Breton’s post noted they have had access to the feature for weeks and described it as a game changer. It remains unclear whether the application will process sent messages through connected apps just once or periodically refer back to them to adapt the user's style over time, and PCMag was unable to activate the feature during its own testing.
