═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Twenty-one-year-old student Surendra Singh Choudhry, known on social media as Bittu Tabahi, is looking to expand his river clean-up efforts to other Indian states after spending months largely by himself clearing plastic and waste from the Ajnar River in Madhya Pradesh. Bittu undertook the initiative using his own resources and without backing from major government campaigns or established organizations, documenting the before-and-after transformation online. His individual effort eventually caught the attention of Dutch environmentalist Boyan Slat, leading to a job offer from Slat's organization, The Ocean Cleanup, which tackles plastic pollution in oceans and rivers. Following the job offer, Bittu posted an Instagram video thanking his followers for their support and asking them to decide his next destination. In the video, Bittu said, "You guys have supported me a lot through this journey of cleaning rivers," and added, "I would like to visit different states now to clean up there as well. I just wanted to ask you guys, which state should I come to and clean up next?" His appeal led followers to suggest locations including Bihar, Uttarakhand, and Maharashtra.
