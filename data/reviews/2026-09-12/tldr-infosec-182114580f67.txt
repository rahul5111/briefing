═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

vLLM implementation work on AMD Instinct MI355X hardware using MiniMax M3 achieved a 3.14 times increase in output throughput for standard serving at concurrency 32, rising from 109.1 to 342.4 output tokens per second per GPU. Public results from the SemiAnalysis InferenceX benchmark show that at concurrency 128, the same four GPU path rose from 297.8 to 623.7 output tokens per second per GPU, representing a 2.09 times improvement. Median time to first token fell from 3.53 to 1.54 seconds, and mean time per output token fell from 100.7 to 48.8 milliseconds at concurrency 128. For MXFP4 quantization, throughput at concurrency 128 rose from 212.1 to 716.8 output tokens per second per GPU under a four GPU contract, while a later two GPU configuration reached 943.5 output tokens per second per GPU. The addition of EAGLE3 speculative decoding reached 682.4 output tokens per second per GPU at concurrency 128, and prefill-decode disaggregation reached 6,370.5 total tokens per second per GPU at concurrency 512 with a 1.32 second median time to first token. The optimization process followed a systematic workflow of estimating dominant costs, measuring them, batching repeated work, precomputing invariants, and moving up the stack when leaf profiles flattened. MiniMax M3 features 60 decoder layers, with 57 utilizing sparse mixture of experts and sparse attention. Engineers addressed localized tensor parallel shapes, fused shared experts into the routed expert table, and integrated AITER sparse paged attention across layout mismatches by turning 128-token blocks into eight 16-token page entries without copying KV data. Correctness audits also resolved bugs such as an unpatched FP8 KV view on FNUZ ROCm devices that initially scored 0.0099 strict match on GSM8K before a patched path scored 0.9575.
