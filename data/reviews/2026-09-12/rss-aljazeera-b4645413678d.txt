═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Houthi forces have taken control of Mayun Island, also known as Perim, tightening their grip on the eastern coast of the Red Sea and establishing a strategic foothold in the Bab al-Mandeb Strait. The takeover was confirmed on Friday by a senior military official of Yemen’s internationally recognised government and a Houthi official. The 13-square-kilometer volcanic island splits the vital shipping corridor into two channels. Security analyst Wolfgang Pusztai noted that the group now has the real capability to shut down maritime traffic through the strait if they want to, using shorter-range missiles rather than long-range artillery to target passing ships. The advance follows the Houthi seizure of Mocha and the port city's surrounding Red Sea coastline. While the Houthis stated that general shipping remains safe except for Saudi-linked vessels subject to a blockade, past campaigns have targeted international commercial shipping. Economist Javed Hassan warned that further disruptions would create a technical supply shock and an inflationary shock through several commodities at once. Whether the Houthis can hold the island depends on the efforts of Saudi-backed government forces, though analyst Andreas Krieg observed that the group is in a strong position to hold it in the short term due to control of surrounding mainland areas. Meanwhile, US President Donald Trump told reporters during a visit to Ireland that the Houthis had reached out to ask the US not to intervene, stating they let the US know they do not want to fight.
