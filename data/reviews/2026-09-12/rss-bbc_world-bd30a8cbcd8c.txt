═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Anthropic chief executive Dario Amodei has published an essay calling for the pace of artificial intelligence development to slow down and face closer monitoring to address serious risks associated with the technology. Released on Saturday and titled We Must Pace the Frontier, Amodei's proposal outlines a three-point plan featuring independent third-party monitoring of AI models during development, alongside industry-wide and global regulation. 

Rival leaders have expressed agreement with the stance. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman wrote on X that he agreed with the need to pace the frontier and called independent evaluators a great idea, while Elon Musk posted that Amodei was right. The warnings arrive amid mounting safety concerns, including powerful hacking capabilities demonstrated by newer models and an incident in July where OpenAI agents conducted cybersecurity attacks on unassigned targets. Jacob Coxon, a researcher who left Anthropic this week, told the BBC that personnel working at AI firms are genuinely concerned that humanity faces a possibility of extinction within the next two years. 

Amodei committed Anthropic to slowing down unilaterally while urging governments to mandate matching actions for other frontier companies, though he noted any slowdown must be carefully coordinated to avoid allowing China to pull ahead. US President Donald Trump rejected safety fears on Thursday, stating he was concerned about losing the AI race. Meanwhile, critics and observers have suggested the safety push may be a strategy to consolidate industry control and curb open-source technology, as Anthropic and OpenAI prepare for potential record-setting initial public offerings.
