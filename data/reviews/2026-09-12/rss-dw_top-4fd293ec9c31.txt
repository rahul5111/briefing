═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Iraq confirmed on Saturday that Friday night attacks on a key Saudi oil pipeline were launched from Iraqi territory. Saudi Arabia shut down the pipeline on Friday as a precautionary measure following a drone attack the previous day, stating it would not retaliate to give Baghdad an opportunity to take necessary measures. In response, the Iraqi government dismissed the military commander and police chief in Maysan province on the Iranian border, an area known for Iran-backed Shiite militias, and closed the province's main border crossing. No group has claimed responsibility, and the Popular Mobilization Forces denied involvement. US President Donald Trump stated on Saturday that Iran was probably responsible for the attack. Iraq faces a September 30 deadline to disarm Iran-aligned Shiite armed groups, which coincide with the withdrawal of remaining US-led coalition forces from Iraq, though several militias have stated they will not comply.
