═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Guru Dhaldas Saheb, a leader from the Satnami community and the older brother of Chhattisgarh BJP Minister Guru Khushwant Saheb, joined the opposition Congress on Friday, September 11, 2026. The Satnami community comprises around 13 percent of Chhattisgarh's population and represents the largest Scheduled Castes group in the state, with Mr. Dhaldas’ family belonging to the lineage of Baba Guru Ghasidas, who founded the Satnam Panth in the 19th century. Mr. Dhaldas joined the Congress in the presence of former Chief Minister Bhupesh Baghel at Mr. Baghel's official residence. On X, Mr. Baghel wrote that the beginning had been made and that together they would remove the BJP. On Saturday, September 12, 2026, State Congress president Deepak Baij stated that Mr. Dhaldas’ entry was just the trailer and that more BJP leaders were preparing to join the Congress. Regarding his reasons for the switch, Mr. Dhaldas told journalists that the BJP was not acting according to the principles of Guru Baba Ghasidas, adding that he had discussed the issue with his family and that there were no political differences between them despite him breaking ranks. Their father Guru Baldas Saheb and younger brother Guru Khushwant Saheb had previously left the Congress to join the BJP in 2023 ahead of the State Assembly elections, with Mr. Khushwant Saheb subsequently winning the Arang Assembly constituency on a BJP ticket. Mr. Dhaldas expressed interest in contesting the 2028 Assembly polls if given a chance, stating that if he did not contest, he would not be able to serve the people. He identified Mungeli, a Scheduled Caste-reserved constituency, as his preferred seat, while noting he would contest from Arang if his brother is not fielded by the BJP.
