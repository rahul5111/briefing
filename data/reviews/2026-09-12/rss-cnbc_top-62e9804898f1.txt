═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian stated in New Delhi on Friday that Iran will not surrender to bullying arrogance, asserting that the country has successfully resisted aggression from the United States and Israel. Pezeshkian's remarks came ahead of the BRICS Summit and followed U.S. President Donald Trump's Thursday assertion that Washington had to take military action to prevent Iran from wiping out Israel and hitting U.S. cities. During a bilateral meeting on Friday, Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi urged dialogue and diplomacy to resolve the Middle East conflict and raised concerns about the safety of seafarers. Shipments through the Strait of Hormuz have slowed since the U.S. and Israel began their war on Iran on February 28, stranding vessels and impacting India, which imports nearly 85 percent of its fuel needs. The conflict has resulted in the deaths of 10 Indian seafarers, with two vessels carrying 30 Indian crew members attacked in July. Modi also addressed the BRICS Summit on Saturday, calling the current global order a pyramid of privilege and urging reforms to the UN Security Council and international financial institutions. BRICS members issued a unanimous joint declaration expressing deep concern over the escalation in the Middle East and urging the protection of civilians and civilian infrastructure. On the sidelines of the summit, Iran and the United Arab Emirates held bilateral talks, following a UAE trade and financial pause enacted last month after two ballistic missiles were launched from Tehran toward UAE territorial waters.
