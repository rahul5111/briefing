The author noted that the robot performed tricks including shaking hands, doing a handstand, and leaping into the air, and completed the downhill morning walk with battery capacity to spare. However, during the afternoon commute home, conditions changed as the temperature reached 87 degrees Fahrenheit and the route was mostly uphill.
