═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Kanniyakumari District Collector M. Prathap stated on Friday that construction work on the Vaniyakudi fishing harbour will proceed based on the recommendations of a review team. Speaking at a fishermen grievance redress meeting held at the district collectorate in Nagercoil, Mr. Prathap addressed multiple demands raised by local fishermen regarding infrastructure, welfare, and environmental issues. 

During the meeting, attendees called for a ban on private tourist boat rides in the Manakudi area, the prompt issuance of family and fishermen membership cards, the extension of a groyne in Kovalam, and Coastal Regulation Zone exemptions for fishermen's residences. Additional petitions requested the construction of a rest shed in Kurumpanai, measures to stop Colachel municipal sewage from entering the AVM Canal, proper rent collection from shops, and improved berthing facilities for country boats at the Thengaipattinam fishing harbour. Fishermen also expressed concern over the alleged smuggling of subsidised kerosene to neighboring states.

In response, the Collector announced that action is being taken to prohibit private tourist boat rides in Manakudi and that strict measures will be enforced against subsidised kerosene smuggling. He stated that camps will be organized to issue membership cards, family cards will be distributed based on seniority, and a proposal has been submitted to extend the Kovalam groyne. Furthermore, Mr. Prathap confirmed that steps will be taken to grant CRZ exemptions for fishermen's homes, prevent sewage discharge into the AVM Canal from Colachel municipality, refix shop rents, and establish berthing facilities at Thengaipattinam. The meeting was also attended by District Revenue Officer M. Velumani and Deputy Director of Fisheries and Fishermen Welfare K.T. Gopinath.
