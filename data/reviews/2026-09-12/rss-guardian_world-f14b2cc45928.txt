═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

King Oyo Nyimba Kabamba Iguru Rukidi IV, who made global headlines in 1995 when he took the Tooro throne at age three, has been buried at the Karambi Royal Tombs in western Uganda. Oyo died last month at 34 in the United States while receiving treatment for cancer. The burial took place 31 years to the day after his coronation and followed a bitter succession dispute between his immediate family, who argued a hitherto unknown son should succeed him according to his will, and a royal committee that chose his 56-year-old cousin, news anchor and editor Edward Rukidi Kijanangoma. 

Kijanangoma marked the formal handover of power by casting nine coffee berries into the grave. Ugandan authorities successfully pushed through his succession on Friday following crisis talks. Royal guards clad in orange shawls led the hearse through the kingdom capital of Fort Portal as thousands of mourners joined the procession on foot. At a preceding church service, Oyo was eulogised by his sister, Ruth Komuntale, who said that although he carried responsibilities far beyond what most people experience, he never completely lost the young boy who loved to have fun. The new king inherits a royal estate including swathes of land and sizeable cash payouts from the central government. Uganda retains several traditional kingdoms that pre-date the British colonial era.
