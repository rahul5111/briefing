═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

President Donald Trump's childhood home in the Jamaica Estates neighborhood of Queens, New York, has sold for $1.93 million, or about 18.45 crore rupees. The Tudor-style house, where Trump lived until he was four years old, was built in 1940 by his father, real estate developer Fred Trump. 

The property was sold following a major eight-month renovation led by developer Tommy Lin, whose identity as the buyer was not disclosed alongside the sale reported after a July contract. Lin purchased the neglected property last year for $835,000, roughly 7.98 crore rupees, after it had stood vacant and suffered water damage, mold, and an infestation of feral cats following a burst pipe. 

Lin spent an additional $500,000, about 4.78 crore rupees, to overhaul the five-bedroom, three-bathroom residence, adding a chef's kitchen, herringbone wood flooring, and spa-style bathrooms. 

The house has changed hands multiple times since Trump became president. In 2017, it sold for $2.14 million to an entity called Trump Birth Home LLC and was subsequently offered on Airbnb with Trump-themed decor. It was listed again in 2019 for $2.9 million but failed to find a buyer, eventually falling into disrepair prior to Lin's purchase.
