═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Cohere and Cohere Labs have released North Small Translate, an open weights sparse Mixture-of-Express research model specializing in high-quality machine translation across 50 languages. 

The decoder-only Transformer model features 25 billion active parameters, 218 billion total parameters, 128 total experts with 8 activated per token, and shared experts applied to every token. It supports a context length of 16K input and 16K output tokens. The architecture uses a 3:1 ratio of sliding-window attention layers with Rotary Positional Embeddings to global attention layers without positional embeddings. 

According to evaluation data from the launch blog post, the model scores 83.60 on WMT26 performance across evaluated languages, increasing to 84.36 when using an agentic multi-pass translation workflow. 

Accessing the repository on Hugging Face requires users to agree to share their contact information, accept the CC BY-NC 4.0 non-commercial license, and adhere to Cohere Labs' Acceptable Use Policy. The release aims to make community-based research efforts more accessible, with commercial use requiring contact with Cohere's sales team.
