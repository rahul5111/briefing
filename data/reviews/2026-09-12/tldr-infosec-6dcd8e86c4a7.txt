═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

LG has strongly denied claims made in a recent YouTube investigation by Gamers Nexus alleging that the manufacturer operates 216 million spy TVs that constantly log and upload user data. The two-hour video, produced in collaboration with security researchers MrBruh and uturn using packet capture and firmware analysis, claims LG smart TVs record audio in standby mode, scan Wi-Fi networks, and sample inputs while offline.

In a statement provided to Tom's Hardware, LG called the claims not true and stated that LG TVs process voice data only when the voice button on the remote control is pressed and held, or when a wake word such as Hi LG is recognized after the user has activated the Far-Field voice recognition feature. The company added that the TVs do not collect or record ambient conversations outside of these instances, and that wake word audio processing is performed locally on the device and immediately deleted if the wake word is not recognized. 

LG confirmed that its smart TVs scan for devices on the same local network, which it described as a standard function of smart TVs and smart home devices. The company also stated that Automatic Content Recognition is an opt-in feature used to deliver personalized recommendations and ads, and is not used for advertising purposes without explicit user consent.
