═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman stated during an interview with Fortune editor in chief Alyson Shontell that the artificial intelligence company will not pursue an initial public offering in 2026. Despite OpenAI having previously filed confidentially for an IPO, Altman dismissed a 2026 public debut as ill advised given current safety concerns and broader technological developments in society. We are not rushing into an IPO, Altman said, adding that right now would be an ill advised moment to go public due to everything happening with safety. Altman indicated that the company will instead go public when both the business is ready and the societal moment for the technology is right, explicitly confirming the timeline is not 2026. The New York Times reported in June that OpenAI had previously hired bankers and lawyers aiming for a public offering in the third or fourth quarter of 2026, though the company was already leaning toward 2027 amid tech stock volatility and financial challenges.
