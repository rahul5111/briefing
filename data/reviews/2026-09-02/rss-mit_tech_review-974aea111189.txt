═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Jabil, a global manufacturing company headquartered in St. Petersburg, Florida, is undergoing an enterprise technology transformation focused on reducing complexity and integrating systems across its global operations. With a footprint of more than 100 sites across 30-plus countries, 140,000-plus employees, and serving more than 400 top global brands, the 60-year-old company faced significant technical debt from legacy applications, spreadsheets, manual workarounds, and site-specific tools built over 25 years. 

According to Harish Manohar, SAP IT director at Jabil, these disconnected systems created data silos that limited the company's ability to spot issues early and coordinate responses across plants and regions. To address this, Jabil adopted a simplify-first, then-innovate mindset, using SAP as its core digital platform and leveraging SAP Business Technology Platform and Integration Suite alongside an API-driven, event-based integration approach. The company is also utilizing SAP Signavio for business process management and moving systems into RISE to pursue a clean-core journey that reduces heavy system customizations. 

The primary driver behind the integration effort is establishing a single, trusted data backbone to enable seamless data flow before the company implements advanced technologies. Any innovation without simplification risks adding more complexity, Manohar notes, stating that "any innovation without simplification is going to add more complexity" and emphasizing that "simplicity at scale is a very competitive advantage." By standardizing processes and connecting supply chains end-to-end, Jabil aims to improve real-time visibility into supply chain events, reduce manual data reconciliation, and lay the groundwork for future AI-driven planning, forecasting, and predictive insights.
