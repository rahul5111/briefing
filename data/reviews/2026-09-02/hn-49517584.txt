1. Compute limitations currently restrict access to frontier LLMs like Fable or Sol for billions of people.
2. By the year 2040, the world may possess the equivalent of eight billion global GPUs.
3. The organization is hosting a story contest offering total prizes worth $100,000 for participants.
4. The first place winner of the contest will receive a cash prize of $40,000.
5. The deadline for submitting entries to the contest is 11:59 PM PT on October 31, 2026.
6. Submissions must be between 1000 and 5000 words in length and formatted as Markdown or PDF.

═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A new story contest is offering one hundred thousand dollars in total prizes to explore what society looks like by the year 2040 when hardware scaling and optimization make the performance equivalent of a B300 GPU available globally to every human being 24/7/365. Only a few million GPUs capable of efficiently serving frontier models like Fable or Sol are currently manufactured annually, leaving billions of humans without access due to compute limitations. The contest invites fiction or nonfiction entries between one thousand and five thousand words envisioning this mundane future where the singularity does not happen but GPUs keep getting made. First prize is forty thousand dollars, second prize is twenty thousand dollars, third prize is twelve thousand dollars, and seven finalist prizes will award four thousand dollars each. Submissions are open to everyone with a limit of one entry per person, and must be licensed under Creative Commons attribution noncommercial or a freer license to allow republication. The deadline for entries is 11:59 PM Pacific Time on October 31, 2026. While the use of large language models is permitted, organizers note it is discouraged because unskillful use tends to reduce originality and writing quality, and participants are requested to disclose any AI use.
