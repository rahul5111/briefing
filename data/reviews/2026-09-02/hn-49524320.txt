═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Movie Scene Map is a free interactive atlas containing 15,716 real filming locations across 166 countries, featuring studios, castles, streets, and landscapes. The platform covers 9,295 films and television series with dedicated pages, alongside 2,153 video games, 407 anime, and 365 manga. Because drawn and rendered worlds are not physically filmed, games, anime, and manga are placed on the map according to where their stories are set, using narrative locations from Wikidata and setting categories from Wikipedia. 

The atlas is built using open data, anchored by filming location statements on Wikidata joined with geographic coordinates, Wikimedia Commons photographs, and Wikipedia articles. It also includes places named within a production's Wikipedia article or its setting categories, with these entries explicitly labeled to distinguish them from direct Wikidata statements. The data is unscrubbed from listicles and contains no generated content. 

Coverage is tied to Wikidata and remains uneven, meaning certain famous productions lack location statements or feature coarse data that references entire countries as text rather than mapping specific pins. Users can add missing information upstream by updating a production's Wikidata item with a source, allowing the entry to appear upon the next data rebuild. 

The atlas operates without accounts, advertising, or paywalls. The entire dataset is free to download under a CC0 license in GeoJSON and CSV formats, and a read-only Model Context Protocol endpoint is available to answer queries live for artificial intelligence assistants.
