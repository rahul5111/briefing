═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Today marks the release of Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, the third Flash release in six weeks. Gemini 3.8 Flash is positioned as an intelligent workhorse model optimized for software engineering, agentic tasks, and multi-step reasoning, maintaining the pricing of its predecessor at seventy-five cents per million input tokens and three dollars and seventy-five cents per million output tokens. The model achieves a score of fifty-four point nine percent on HLE-Verified and outperforms previous iterations across specialized benchmarks including Vals Finance Agent V2 and Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark. Accompanying the standard release is Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a specialized cybersecurity variant designed for vulnerability detection and automated patching. On the CWE-Bench patching benchmark, the cyber variant achieves a pass-at-one score of forty-seven point two percent. Google reports real-world implementations, noting that the Chrome Security team generated two point six times more correct patches using the cyber model compared to larger commercial models, and the Cloud Vulnerability Research team utilized the tool to identify a critical foundational vulnerability in under two hours. While Gemini 3.8 Flash is widely available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and enterprise and consumer applications, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is restricted to trusted government authorities, critical infrastructure operators, and software maintainers through the newly introduced Fairwind Program.
