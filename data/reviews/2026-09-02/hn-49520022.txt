═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The open-source Android flashcard application AnkiDroid faces removal from the Google Play Store on September 11, 2026, worldwide except for India and Russia, unless it removes its donation link. Google has rejected app updates since August 28 under its payments policy, which restricts external billing links unless directed to a validated tax-exempt organization. 

AnkiDroid, which has over 10 million installs and relies on donations to its fiscal host Open Source Collective for maintenance and development, submitted an IRS determination letter showing the collective holds tax-exempt status under Section 501(c)(6). Google rejected the documentation, stating in an email that the app "allows users to contribute donations to an organization that is not tax-exempt," without explaining why the 501(c)(6) determination is insufficient. 

The development team announced they are removing the donation links immediately in version 2.24.X under protest, stating, "we are doing so under protest, as otherwise we would be unable to distribute AnkiDroid to the majority of our users who use the Play Store."
