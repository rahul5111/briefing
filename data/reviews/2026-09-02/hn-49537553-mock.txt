═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Today, the release of Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber introduces two new variants built on a shared reasoning and coding core, marking the third Flash release in six weeks. Gemini 3.8 Flash is designed as an intelligent workhorse model optimized for software engineering, agentic tasks, and multi-step reasoning. It is available at an introductory price of $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens, matching the pricing of 3.7 Flash. On benchmarks such as DeepSWE v1.1, Vals Finance Agent V2, and Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark, the model outperforms previous versions and several larger frontier models, while achieving 54.9% on HLE-Verified. The model utilizes extra reasoning steps and iterative tool calls to maximize performance on complex tasks, though developers can adjust effort levels or use 3.7 Flash for efficiency-first workloads. The second variant, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, is a specialized cybersecurity model focused on vulnerability detection and automated patching. On the CyberGym benchmark and an internal benchmark spanning 20 programming languages, the model achieved a success rate exceeding 70% in autonomous vulnerability discovery. On the CWE-Bench patching benchmark, it recorded a pass@1 of 47.2%. The model is already deployed across Google, where the Chrome Security team found it produced 2.6 times more correct patches than larger commercial models, and Google’s Cloud Vulnerability Research team used it to find a critical foundational vulnerability in less than two hours. Due to its capabilities, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber features a more permissive set of mitigations and is restricted to trusted defenders through the new Fairwind Program, which grants access to government authorities, critical infrastructure operators, and software maintainers. Meanwhile, standard 3.8 Flash is available immediately for developers, enterprise customers, and AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.
