═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenTIE and OpenXWA have been launched as new open-source reimplementations of the classic space combat simulators Star Wars: TIE Fighter and Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance for Windows, macOS, and Linux. OpenTIE runs original game data natively on current systems, supporting both the 1995 Collector's CD-ROM edition and the 1998 Windows release. The software requires a complete installation of at least one supported edition, which are available from GOG and Steam, as it does not include original game content. 

With both editions installed, OpenTIE allows players to combine the 1995 menus, cutscenes, and adaptive iMUSE soundtrack with the 1998 flight simulation and 3D assets. The project also reimplements the iMUSE soundtrack to respond dynamically to mission events, alongside built-in emulation options for the Roland SC-55 Sound Canvas, Sound Blaster OPL3 FM-synth, and FluidSynth using user-supplied SoundFonts. 

OpenTIE introduces modern technical upgrades including high-resolution rendering, advanced lighting features such as shadows, ambient occlusion, bloom, motion blur, HDR output, and MSAA alongside AMD FidelityFX FSR 3.1.4 anti-aliasing and upscaling. Players can toggle between classic and modern graphics modes by pressing TAB during gameplay. Flight simulation updates at up to 240 Hz to support modern high-refresh-rate displays, while retaining original TIE95 and TIE98 timing modes for users preferring exact historical behavior. The engine also features native support for modern gamepads and joysticks. 

Development packages and platform-specific instructions are available on GitHub, requiring a 64-bit system with a modern GPU, CMake 3.23 or later, a C/C++ toolchain, SDL3 3.4, zstd, FFmpeg, and SDL_shadercross. Users can join the TotallyOpen Discord server to discuss OpenTIE and OpenXWA development.
