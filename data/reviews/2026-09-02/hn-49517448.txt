═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Fastpotify is a new native binary Spotify client built without a browser engine that starts in well under a second and typically uses 100 to 250 megabytes of RAM. The application allows users to play music locally, gapless, and at up to 320 kilobits per second, or control playback on a speaker, phone, or television from the same window. Users can browse playlists, liked songs, albums, artists, and podcasts, as well as search the catalog and edit playlists they own. The interface supports light, dark, or system modes, with pages and the player bar able to adapt their color from album art. Additional features include keyboard shortcuts, Linux MPRIS media controls, and a tray option that keeps music playing after the window is closed. Users can also load classic Winamp skin files from the Winamp Skin Museum into a mini player equipped with a spectrum analyzer, equalizer, playlist, shade modes, and crisp integer pixel scaling. Upon first use, Fastpotify automatically downloads the original MilkDrop 2 presets and projectM Cream of the Crop collection, which react to local playback in either a resizable window or fullscreen mode.
