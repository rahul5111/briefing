Google has announced the release of Gemini 3.8 Flash, marking the company's third Flash model rollout in a span of six weeks. Despite not releasing a frontier-level Gemini Pro model since early 2026, Google describes the new release as its best reasoning and coding model yet. Gemini 3.8 Flash is available in two variations, including a standard workhorse model designed for tasks ranging from agentic workflows to software development, and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, which is specifically tuned for vulnerability detection and mitigation. For developers utilizing API access, Google has introduced an introductory pricing rate through the end of the year set at $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens.
