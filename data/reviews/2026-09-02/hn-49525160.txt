═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

World Labs has introduced Atlas, its next-generation omni world model pretrained from scratch to natively operate on text, images, video, and 3D. The model is a multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer that combines all inputs into a shared spatial context to generate consistent 3D environments, supporting tasks such as camera-controlled generation up to one minute of video at 1440p resolution, spatial reconstruction, space-time simulation, and image generation. Built to scale, Atlas allows users to place reference images in 3D space to smoothly interpolate between them, reconstruct real-world scenes from sparse inputs ranging from one to over a hundred images, and output explicit 3D representations like point clouds and 3D Gaussian splats. The model also powers bullet-time multiview capture using footage from as few as three cell phones and scales Real-to-Sim workflows for robotics by simulating sensor data and object interactions from casual recordings. Atlas will power future versions of Marble and other World Labs products.
