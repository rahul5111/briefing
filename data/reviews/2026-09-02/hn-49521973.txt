═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Mozilla has introduced a built-in Ad Blocker for Firefox on iOS designed to block many third-party ads and ad-related trackers before they load. The tool utilizes Apple’s WebKit Content Blocker technology alongside the EasyList filter list. 

The feature is built directly into the browser because extensions are not available on iOS in the same way they are on desktop and Android. Users can enable the feature by navigating to Settings, selecting Browsing, and then Ad Blocker. The tool is turned off by default. 

The ad blocker will not block every ad. Advertisements served directly by the visited site, ads appearing in search results, and sponsored shortcuts or content shown when opening a new tab will not be affected. The ad blocker functions alongside Firefox's existing privacy protections, including Enhanced Tracking Protection.
