═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Gurugram Police are examining viral helmet-camera footage of a road-rage incident on Golf Course Road in which a white sedan allegedly pursued and rammed a woman motorcyclist on Sunday morning. The rider, who runs the Instagram account rebelwheels_sia_ and identifies as Sia, was riding an Aprilia sports bike with friends when the car driver came dangerously close and signaled her to stop. According to Sia, the car rammed her motorcycle, throwing her onto the road where she was dragged for several feet before the driver fled. She also alleged that the car occupants were drunk. Gurugram Police public relations officer Sandeep Turan stated that no immediate written complaint or police control room alert was filed following the crash. Turan told Hindustan Times that police are verifying the exact crash location using the video to establish jurisdiction. Police stated they will attempt to contact the rider to obtain a formal complaint and register a first information report to arrest the driver.
