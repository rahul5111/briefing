═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Coway released the Airmega Halo at the end of August as a lightweight, affordable air purifier designed for medium to large rooms and focused on sleep features and quiet operation. The device weighs 9.7 pounds, features a cylindrical 360-degree air-intake structure, and utilizes Coway's HyperVortex filtration system, an aerodynamic fan and duct design. The Halo follows the March release of the Coway Airmega Mighty2 and the long-running Airmega Mighty. Field-testing revealed some drawbacks, including a lack of a top handle or wheels that made the device awkward to move, the absence of Wi-Fi connectivity, apps, or a remote, and reliance on colored lights rather than transparent numbers for pollutant levels.
