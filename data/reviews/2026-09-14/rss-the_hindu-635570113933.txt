═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Jharkhand Governor Santosh Kumar Gangwar and Chief Minister Hemant Soren on Monday, September 14, 2026, greeted the people of the state on the occasion of Ganesh Chaturthi, praying for peace and prosperity. The festival is being celebrated with traditional fervour across the state amid heightened security, with major puja committees opening their pandals to devotees starting Sunday evening, September 13. Posting on X, Governor Gangwar wrote, "May Lord Shri Ganesh bestow happiness, peace, and prosperity in everyone's life and remove all obstacles, making life auspicious." Chief Minister Soren also shared greetings on X, stating, "May Lord Shri Ganesh remove all impediments and hurdles from everyone's life and bless us with happiness, peace, prosperity, and success. May love, harmony, and joy prevail among all—this is my heartfelt wish." To ensure safety during the celebrations, Jharkhand police made adequate security arrangements at major puja pandals in Ranchi as well as other cities including Dhanbad and Jamshedpur, according to a police official.
