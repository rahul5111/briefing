═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The United States has designated the Ecuadorian gang Los Tiguerones as a terrorist organization as part of an expanding Trump administration campaign against narcotics traffickers. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced the designation in Quito during a meeting with Ecuadorian President Daniel Noboa on a three-nation tour that began on Tuesday. Rubio stated that the gang is linked to narcotics trafficking, attacks on civilians, law enforcement officers, and journalists, and described them as terrorists possessing weapons and equipment resembling national armies. Los Tiguerones previously gained international attention in 2024 when members stormed a live broadcast at the TC Televisión network, prompting Noboa to declare a state of internal armed conflict. Rubio said the Trump administration would seek 45 million dollars in additional funding from Congress to dismantle terrorist groups in Ecuador, which he hailed as the administration's most aggressive security partner in the region. The designation falls under the Shield of the Americas coalition, an initiative aimed at tightening security cooperation with rightwing allies to fight narcotics trafficking and illegal migration. During his tour, Rubio also visited Colombia on Tuesday to meet its newly inaugurated president, Abelardo De La Espriella, with whom they signed memorandums of understanding on critical minerals and nuclear energy. Rubio is scheduled to travel next to Peru to meet President Keiko Fujimori as part of the regional tour engaging newly elected conservative leaders.
