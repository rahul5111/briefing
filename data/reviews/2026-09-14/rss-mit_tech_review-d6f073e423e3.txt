═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Major artificial intelligence companies are entering the healthcare sector to help process clinical records, interpret terminology, and reduce clinician cognitive burden by organizing fragmented data. However, healthcare leaders face a deeper operational challenge rooted in fragmented information, workflows, and accountability across electronic health records, billing platforms, and payer portals. The revenue cycle is becoming a primary proving ground for healthcare AI because it combines high transaction volumes, complex reasoning, and measurable financial outcomes, though generic automation and foundation models often fall short due to changing payer requirements and evolving documentation expectations. While large language models improve the extraction of meaning from narrative text, they lack native awareness of local workflow constraints and proprietary operational data. To bridge this gap, Ensemble developed a revenue cycle intelligence engine called EIQ. EIQ uses a neuro-symbolic approach that combines large and small language models with rules-based reasoning, integrating operational activity, clinical documentation, payer behavior, and reimbursement outcomes with hospital electronic health record systems. This architecture relies on a decade of operational performance, transaction history, and operator decision-making to apply guardrails and ensure traceability. The next decade of healthcare AI will be defined by integration with governed data and operational workflows rather than model capability alone.
