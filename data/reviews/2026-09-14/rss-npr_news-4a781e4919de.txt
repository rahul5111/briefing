═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Indonesian authorities on Monday intensified the search for 129 people still missing after the ferry Virgo Transport 8 overturned in the Java Sea. At least six passengers were killed in the disaster, while 108 people were rescued following the incident on Sunday. The 119-meter vessel was carrying 243 people, comprising 213 passengers and 30 crew members, along with 89 vehicles when it capsized in Masalembo waters about 148 kilometers from Banjarmasin. Traveling from Surabaya in East Java province to Banjarmasin on Borneo Island, the captain issued a distress call around 2 a.m. Sunday reporting the ship was listing in rough seas with waves up to three meters before contact was lost. Mohammad Syafii, the National Search and Rescue Agency chief, said 17 ships and aircraft carrying about 622 personnel from the military, police, and other agencies were deployed for the search, alongside specialized underwater teams and navy-operated drones. We remain hopeful of finding more survivors, Syafii told a video briefing. Distraught relatives gathered at command centers in Banjarmasin and Surabaya to await news of unaccounted family members. Ferries are a common method of travel across Indonesia's archipelago of more than 17,000 islands, though disasters occur regularly due in part to weak enforcement of safety standards.
