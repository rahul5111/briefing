═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Athletics has completed its inaugural Ultimate Championship in Budapest, a three-day event featuring three successive sell-out crowds and a record ten million dollar prize pot distributed among the sport's biggest stars. Developed over three years by World Athletics and president Lord Coe to address the lack of a consistent season-ending pinnacle event, the competition utilized an innovative format designed to engage larger audiences and compensate athletes more closely aligned with other sports. The event introduced a striking visual appearance, including an infield painted black and a pink sandpit for horizontal jumps, alongside TV enhancements such as live win probabilities. However, the event faced criticism for omitting several disciplines based on viewer metrics, including distance track events like the ten thousand meters and all shot put and discus events. Usain Bolt, event ambassador and former sprinter, said it was a good first step towards changing track and field for the better. British fifteen hundred meters champion Josh Kerr, who earned a significantly increased pay cheque at the event, noted he welcomes anything that can push the sport to be more entertaining. The biennial championship is scheduled to return in 2028 following the Olympics, though questions remain regarding its long-term placement alongside the established Diamond League schedule.
