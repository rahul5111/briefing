═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Insight Partners co-head Deven Parekh discussed the firm’s $90 billion portfolio, its strategy on frontier AI labs, and market concentration during a sit-down with TechCrunch at the StrictlyVC event in New York on Thursday night. 

Addressing the massive influx of capital into frontier AI, Parekh noted that while some peers are concentrating 35 to 40 percent of their funds into OpenAI and Anthropic, Insight maintains a diversified strategy. He warned that data over time does not support excessive concentration and that the venture business rewards a long-term horizon across multiple funds. Insight holds stakes in both OpenAI and Anthropic, which Parekh defended by explaining that at later stages off the board, investors are simply buying great stock rather than driving governance. 

Parekh also highlighted Insight's liquidity performance, stating that the firm has returned more than $20 billion to limited partners over the last two years through strategic sales and IPOs. On broader market conditions, he observed that startup valuations are rising at a pace reminiscent of 2021, prompting Insight to write smaller early-stage checks—such as $20 million to $25 million instead of $500 million—to reduce risk. He noted that the firm has not executed a major buyout since 2024 due to high interest rates and unyielding debt markets.
