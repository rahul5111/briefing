═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Ramanuj Goswami, an Assam man who beheaded his wife earlier this month, died early Monday morning after jumping from a moving police vehicle. Police sources said Goswami sustained a severe head injury while being transported to Gauhati Medical College and Hospital for a medical examination on Narakasur hill. He was scheduled to appear in court on Monday following the conclusion of a seven-day police custody. Goswami had married Riya Talukdar on September 22 last year. On September 3, following a heated argument over an alleged extramarital affair, Goswami killed Talukdar, beheaded her, and stored the severed head inside a refrigerator. He surrendered to police on September 6, at which point officers recovered the body and head. While being transported for a medical examination on September 8, Goswami told the media he had no regret for the crime, would not seek an apology, and vowed that he would kill the victim's sugar daddy if he ever gets out of jail. According to sources, Goswami was a cab driver who lived with his wife in a flat in the Beltola area of Guwahati and had previously undergone treatment for substance use at a rehab center.
