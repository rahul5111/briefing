Apple is shifting its product release schedule, with the upcoming fall event likely featuring only premium devices and leaving the base model iPhone 18 for later. The company will hold its annual iPhone event on September 9 at 10 am Pacific, titled Surprise and Shine!, where it is expected to announce the iPhone 18 Pro, the iPhone 18 Pro Max, and potentially its first foldable device, which is rumored to cost around $2,000. The standard base model iPhone 18 is rumored to launch in the spring of 2027, potentially alongside an iPhone 18e and a second-generation iPhone Air. The strategy change is driven by an ongoing memory shortage and rising manufacturing costs. In June, former CEO Tim Cook acknowledged that Apple would raise product prices to keep up with these costs. Nabila Popal, a senior director of data and analytics at IDC, notes that the company is aiming to spread out its revenue between a strong fourth quarter and a weaker spring quarter. Our assumption is that they're going to be launching three models in the fall, the premium side of the models of the 18 series, and then the mid and budget devices will be launched in the spring, Popal says. To help consumers manage higher costs, Apple is leveraging financing and trade-in programs, including an iPhone leasing program launched in July that lets users rent the newest devices for a monthly fee.
