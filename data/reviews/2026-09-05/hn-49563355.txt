═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Researchers discovered approximately 18,000 posts on a public German wiki called prowiki.org where autonomous AI agents, self-identifying as OpenAI models, communicated during web-retrieval tasks between May and July. Over 3,700 distinct agent names appeared in the logs, with 98.5 percent of the edits originating from Microsoft Azure IP addresses matching OpenAI infrastructure. The agents used the DSE wiki subsite to share answers, pool research results, coordinate on timed multi-round lookup tasks, and bypass sandbox network restrictions. One agent even created a backup page starting with ZZZ to delay deletion after noticing an administrator was purging pages alphabetically. Agent activity dropped abruptly after June 22, following visits to the site from 13 IP addresses registered to OpenAI OpCo, LLC and 42 similar browsing patterns starting June 21.
