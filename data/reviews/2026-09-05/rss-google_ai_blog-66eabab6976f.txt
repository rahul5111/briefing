═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google announced a series of major AI updates and hardware releases in August 2026, highlighted by the Gemini app officially surpassing one billion monthly users as the fastest-growing product in the company's history. Usage milestones revealed that 63 percent of users talk directly to Gemini, while the platform generates more than 150 million images every day. 

In developer tools and hardware, Google released the Gemini 3.7 Flash workhorse model for coding and agents at half the introductory price of 3.6 Flash per million tokens. The company also unveiled the Pixel 11 series—including the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold—powered by the new Google Tensor G6 chip and designed to run Gemini Nano. Additional technical releases included the Gemini 3.5 Transcribe speech-to-text model, the Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash video generation tool featuring 4K upscaling, and the open-sourcing of the WeatherNext 2 model, which predicts cyclone tracks with state-of-the-art accuracy. 

To support education, Google offered one year of a Google AI plan free to eligible college students worldwide alongside new SAT prep and interactive learning features in Search. In enterprise and climate initiatives, Gemini Live added productivity features such as Personal Intelligence and Daily Brief, while Operation Blue Skies expanded AI-powered contrail-reduction flight routing through a partnership with the UK Government and aviation leaders.
