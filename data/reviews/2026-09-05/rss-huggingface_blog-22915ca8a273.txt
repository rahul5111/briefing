═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

IBM and Confluent are partnering to bring real-time time series intelligence to streaming data through the integration of IBM Granite Time Series models and Confluent Cloud. The offering enables native model inference directly within Apache Flink on Confluent, allowing teams to run forecasting and anomaly detection on live data without provisioning separate machine learning infrastructure or moving data to a data warehouse.

The integration provides zero configuration, built-in governance, and cost efficiency by eliminating cloud ingress and egress fees and running natively on CPUs. Access to the capabilities opens on Confluent Cloud on AWS, with Confluent Platform following for on-premises and hybrid environments. 

The offering includes four complementary time series foundation models available in Early Access, which can be called through Confluent AI_FORECAST and AI_DETECT_ANOMALIES Flink SQL functions. The portfolio includes PatchTST-FM, FlowState, TTM, and TSPulse, which together back a range of use cases from demand planning across hundreds of thousands of SKUs to real-time fraud detection and anomaly identification. With over 44 million downloads behind the underlying foundation models, the collaboration aims to compress the time between a live business event and an actionable decision from days to seconds.
