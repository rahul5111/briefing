═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Newly promoted Hull City extended their unbeaten start to the Premier League season with a 0-0 draw against Aston Villa at a sold-out MKM Stadium. The result leaves Hull with seven points from their opening three games and secures their third successive clean sheet under manager Sergej Jakirovic, who signed a two-year contract extension on Friday. Hull came closest to breaking the deadlock in the second half when Mohamed Belloumi hit the crossbar with a curling shot, while substitutes Mohamed-Ali Cho and Oli McBurnie also threatened late in the match. The clean sheet preserves a strong defensive run for the Tigers, who are looking to defy expectations and avoid relegation this campaign. For Aston Villa, the stalemate extended a frustrating scoring drought to three consecutive matches following a 4-0 defeat at Brighton and a 1-0 loss to Arsenal. Villa managed just one shot on target during the match, a tame early effort by George Hemmings, though captain John McGinn and Emiliano Buendía both missed clear opportunities after halftime. Manager Unai Emery acknowledged the team's lack of a clinical edge while pointing to an ongoing squad rebuild following a summer of major player turnover. Reflecting on the performance, Jakirovic said that they could have won but would not be ungrateful for seven points after three games and another clean sheet, adding that it was a great result that showed they can compete at this level. Emery noted that the result was not enough and that they deserved more, explaining that they were not clinical and needed goals to win matches.
