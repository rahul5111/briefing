═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Enumerators have started door-to-door enumeration for Census 2027 starting September 1 in Ladakh, Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, and Uttarakhand, while the Centre has allocated Rs 6,000 crore in the February 1 Union Budget for the NPR and Census 2027. The controversy centers on 14 new or modified questions notified by the Centre on August 14, including details on parents, bank accounts, mobile numbers, Aadhaar, voter ID, passports, and driving licences. Opposition leaders, including Congress MP Jairam Ramesh, former West Bengal chief minister Mamata Banerjee, and former Kerala chief minister Pinarayi Vijayan, have criticized the inclusion of these details, alleging the Census serves as a backdoor for the National Population Register and the National Register of Citizens. Ramesh stated on X that Census 2027 has a deeper nefarious purpose, while Vijayan argued that citizenship cannot be tied to religion and urged the Union Government to drop divisive questions. Legal protections under Section 15 of the Census Act, 1948 prohibit the inspection of census records or their admissibility as evidence in civil or criminal proceedings, preventing the direct use of census data for citizenship determination. However, cyber and privacy law expert Prashant Mali warned that the State cannot bypass statutory barriers through indirect citizenship inquiries, adding that the interaction between the Census Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act will be notable during field operations around May 13, 2027.
