═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Legal and professional operating system Legora used GPT-6 Astra to review 41 documents in a single agent run lasting minutes during a financial-statement tie-out. Legora legal engineer Percevale Perks noted that the workflow normally takes an entire evening or sometimes days. The agent checked every figure in draft accounts against trial balances, a consolidation schedule, and the previous year’s accounts, successfully finding all four planted errors, including a £500,000 gap hidden in the revenue note. Evaluated using the Legora Benchmark for Agentic Reasoning, GPT-6 Astra improved performance by nearly 40% on the financial-statement workflow compared to the previous model, while averaging a 3% improvement across all tasks in the benchmark. Perks stated that the key change between the models is the processing power and the ability to ingest a large number of documents, digest complex information, and handle different line items and figures. Legora uses the agent to handle exhaustive comparisons while keeping human legal professionals responsible for final judgments, supporting the platform's expansion into audit, tax, compliance, and risk. Legora is currently used by more than 100,000 professionals across over 1,800 in-house legal departments and law firms in more than 50 markets.
