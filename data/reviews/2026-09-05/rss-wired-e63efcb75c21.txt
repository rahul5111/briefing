═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Amazon increased prices across much of its Echo smart speaker and display lineup last month, citing rising costs for storage, memory, and chips. The price hikes range from $20 to $100 depending on the model. The Echo Dot fifth generation rose $30 to $80, the Echo Spot second generation went up $30 to $110, the Echo Dot Max increased $20 to $120, the Echo Show 8 fourth generation rose $20 to $200, the Echo Show 11 increased $30 to $250, the Echo Show 15 went up $50 to $350, and the largest increase hit the Echo Show 21, which climbed $100 to $500. 

Alongside the hardware price adjustments, Amazon rolled out Alexa+, a generative AI assistant available via a free 30-day trial followed by either a $20 monthly fee or an Amazon Prime membership. The introduction of Alexa+ also changed privacy settings, requiring all voice recordings to be sent to Amazon for processing. A previous feature allowing local processing of voice requests was discontinued on March 28, 2025.
