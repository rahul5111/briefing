═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google is rolling out a new image creation and editing tool called Google Pics over the coming weeks to all Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and most Workspace business customers. Built on the Nano Banana image generation and editing model, the tool is designed for tasks such as designing posters, producing social media posts, and editing digital illustrations. 

Google Pics will function as both a standalone product and an integrated feature within Workspace applications, starting with Slides, Docs, and Drive. The software includes fine-tuned editing capabilities, featuring object segmentation to isolate and transform specific parts of an image using text comments, in-image text editing and translation that preserves design and fonts, collaborative editing features for shared creations, and multiple generation options that provide several iterations from a single prompt. 

Millions of users interact with billions of images across Workspace products every month. The integration aims to eliminate the need to jump between applications, allowing users to harness AI image generation and editing directly within their existing workflows without copying, pasting, or switching tabs.
