═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Cerebras has made the Qwen 3.8 27B model available on its platform running at a speed of 1500 tokens per second. The platform hosts original, unpruned versions of open-source community models on its public endpoints rather than pruned variants. While Cerebras conducts research on pruning techniques such as Router-weighted Expert Activation Pruning, or REAP, those specific pruned models are shared exclusively with the research community on Hugging Face and are not offered through the shared API. For storage, Cerebras applies selective weight-only quantization using partial 16-bit, 8-bit, and 4-bit formats to reduce memory usage without altering model architecture. To maintain output quality, sensitive layers are stored at full precision and dequantized on the fly so that core operations execute in high precision, while activations, attention, and the KV cache remain entirely unquantized and in full precision.
