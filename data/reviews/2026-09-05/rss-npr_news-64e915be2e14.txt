═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The United States military announced on Saturday that it struck three Iranian oil tankers after Navy warships were targeted with missiles in the region. The U.S. statement said an aircraft carrier and a destroyer evaded multiple unprovoked Iranian attacks while patrolling, and no U.S. personnel were injured. Two of the Iranian oil carriers were permanently disabled, while a third, unladen tanker was destroyed. According to the U.S. military, the targeted vessels were part of a shadow network helping to fund Iran's Revolutionary Guard and its regional armed proxies. 

Iranian state television had earlier reported that four U.S. missiles struck a tanker about six miles or ten kilometers from Kharg Island, which houses a major oil export terminal. The U.S. stated that one tanker was struck off Kharg Island, another near Jask east of the Strait of Hormuz, and the unladen tanker in the Gulf of Oman. The strikes followed a collapse in negotiations and occurred a day after President Donald Trump described the conflict as small potatoes, returning the parties to fighting that began with initial U.S. and Israeli attacks on February 28. 

The head of U.S. Central Command, Adm. Brad Cooper, said, "We will not hesitate to defend American forces, and if necessary, destroy Iran's limited and exposed oil fleet." The military warned it would continue targeting the oil vessels if threats persist. The renewed military engagement follows the breakdown of mid-June talks intended to address Iran's nuclear program and comes as traffic remains low through the strategically vital Strait of Hormuz.
