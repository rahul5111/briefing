═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

An Egyptian court sentenced television presenter Sarah Khalifa, 39, and 11 other defendants to death by hanging for manufacturing and trafficking narcotics, according to official media reports on Saturday. Khalifa is best known for hosting the security and crime programme Mission Impossible and also owns a cosmetic surgery clinic and an event production company. The state-linked Al-Ahram newspaper reported that the Cairo court convicted the group of forming an organised criminal gang that procured and imported materials from abroad to manufacture synthetic drugs, alongside charges of possessing unlicensed firearms and ammunition. Authorities seized more than 750 kilograms of narcotics and raw materials, and discovered two apartments used as laboratories. The verdict followed consultation with the Grand Mufti of Egypt, a mandatory legal procedure for capital cases, and remains subject to appeal. In a separate case, the court sentenced Khalifa and others to five years in prison for kidnapping and assaulting a young man. Under Egyptian law, the death penalty applies to offences including drug trafficking. According to the Egyptian Commission for Rights and Freedoms, 541 death sentences were issued in Egypt in 2025.
