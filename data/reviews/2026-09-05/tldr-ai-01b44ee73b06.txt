═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra has achieved state-of-the-art results on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark, scoring 62.7% for $26,000 using a Standard harness and 99.9% for $19,000 using a Provider Adapter harness on the ARC-AGI-3 Semi-Private test. The benchmark evaluates agentic intelligence through abstract, turn-based environments testing exploration, modeling, goal-setting, and planning without explicit instructions. Astra surpassed the human baseline in action efficiency, utilizing fewer actions than the median tested human on 96% of levels and averaging 51.7% fewer actions per level. During testing, Astra demonstrated the ability to convert unfamiliar environments into compact symbolic world models by creating a custom domain-specific language shorthand to track state, objects, coordinates, and rules. In an evaluation using the PRO-LONG harness with a code interpreter sandbox, Astra generated custom game-specific software libraries including board parsers, search algorithms, and solver scripts. Provider Adapter runs were approximately 3.66 times faster by aggregate recorded elapsed time and used 49% fewer total tokens compared to the Standard harness across the 167 game-reasoning pairs solved by both configurations. While the developers noted that Astra represents a step-function change in frontier capabilities, they stated that saturating the benchmark does not prove the achievement of artificial general intelligence, as ARC-AGI-3 environments feature bounded scope and deterministic mechanics.
