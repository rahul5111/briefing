═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Russian President Vladimir Putin has ordered a three-day halt to strikes on Kyiv, beginning at midnight, in connection with the arrival of a US delegation in Moscow. US special representatives Steve Witkoff and Kushner landed in Moscow on Saturday to meet with Putin and are scheduled to travel to Ukraine on Sunday. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov confirmed the temporary suspension, stating, Putin has ordered that no strikes be carried out against Kyiv for three days, starting at midnight today. The visit comes as Washington pursues a fresh concrete proposal aimed at ending the war, which is now in its fifth year. US President Donald Trump mentioned the new proposal on Friday without providing details. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy stated on Saturday that Ukraine would also halt airstrikes during the envoys visit. The diplomatic push coincides with an intensification of long-range attacks by both sides, including Russian strikes that killed five people overnight and hit Kyiv and Boryspil international airports, alongside a Russian drone strike on the SBU security service headquarters in Kyiv on Friday. Russia and Ukraine remain divided over settlement terms, with the Kremlin maintaining that Putin's demand for Ukraine to cede four claimed regions and abandon NATO membership remains unshakeable.
