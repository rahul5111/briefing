═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

On Wednesday, a crew removed a 24-foot-tall metal sculpture titled Blue, created by the late artist Joel Shapiro, from the grounds of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts in Washington DC. The work had been installed on The REACH, a portion of the Center's grounds that opened in 2019. Describing the sculpture, Shapiro said in a video on the Kennedy Center website, It is about action and risk and performance and energy, and it is a celebration of possibility. Josef Palermo, who was let go in March as the Center's curator of visual arts and special programming, told the New York Times that the sculpture had been targeted early on for removal by the Trump administration. The Kennedy Center issued a statement saying they are deeply grateful to Joel Shapiro for the years Blue has spent animating their campus and connecting visitors to his vision. A representative from the artist's foundation stated that the sculpture will be cleaned, reassembled, and reinstalled in a new location.
