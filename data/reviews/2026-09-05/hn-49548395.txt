═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Audacity 4.0 has been released, featuring a complete interface rebuild on Qt that introduces native high-DPI rendering, customizable toolbars, saved workspaces, and light, dark, and high-contrast themes. The update brings a new clip-editing model that allows users to select, group, move, and trim multiple clips together, alongside a dedicated split tool accessed by pressing or holding the S key. Clips can now move freely between mono and stereo tracks, with overlapping moves replacing the overlapped part rather than blocking the action. The software introduces the new aup4 project format, which opens and converts older aup3 projects without modifying the original files, though converted projects cannot be saved back to aup3. Playback and recording updates allow the playhead to remain visible during navigation, enable seeking without stopping playback, and provide ASIO playback and recording support in official Windows builds. Several Audacity 3 features are currently unavailable in version 4.0, including Time Tracks, Note and MIDI tracks, the Mixer, Macro Manager and scripting pipe, VAMP and LADSPA plugin hosting, and play-at-speed, though developers are working to add them in future releases.
