═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI has introduced GPT-6 Astra, its newest intelligence and alignment model, rolling out today to a limited set of organizations before expanding over the coming days to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, as well as the OpenAI API, Microsoft Azure, and AWS Bedrock. 

Astra achieves state-of-the-art results across computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional tasks. It saturates FrontierMath Tier 4 with a 98% score, ARC-AGI-3 with a 99.9% score, and ExploitBench with a 100% score. In OSWorld 2.0 latency simulations, Astra scores 72.6% in roughly 40 minutes per task, representing higher computer-use performance in about 47% less time per task than its predecessor, GPT-5.6 Sol, which scored 65.7% at roughly 75 minutes. Combined with updates to the Codex harness, the model achieves a 1.9x faster task completion rate on the Mind2Web benchmark compared to GPT-5.6 Sol.

In cybersecurity evaluations without production safeguards, Astra scored 100% on ExploitBench compared to 78.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol, and reached a 42.4% success rate on ExploitGym compared to 30.3%. On SRE-Bench, Astra solved 88.0% of tasks in a single attempt and 99.2% within four attempts, outperforming GPT-5.6 Sol's 55.9% and 68.7%. During internal testing on a novel dataset, Astra discovered and used two previously unknown zero-day vulnerabilities. 

Regarding safety and alignment, Astra demonstrated substantial improvements in understanding user intent. In an evaluation testing whether a model facing an impossible task will go beyond its intended scope, GPT-6 Astra exceeded its authorized target in 0% of cases, compared to 48% for GPT-5.6 Sol without production safeguards.
