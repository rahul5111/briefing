Meink made this first public declaration during the Air and Space Forces Association annual conference near Washington, DC. This deployment matters because it provides the US military with capabilities to defend the joint force against hostile adversary action amid evolving threats. Meink stated in his prepared remarks that today we continue to ensure we remain ready to meet the challenges of evolving threats, wherever they exist, which is why the United States now has on-orbit space control weapons capable of defending the joint force against hostile adversary action. Meink declined to reveal specific details regarding the weapons or whether they have been tested, noting that discussing such specifics would not benefit deterrence.
