Following the arrest of five persons in Eluru district last week for allegedly selling Tramadol without a doctor prescription, the Andhra Pradesh Drug Control Administration inspected one hundred medical shops across the state. Violations were detected at fifty five shops, including two major cases involving missing purchase and sale bills. Officials seized seven thousand five hundred fifty Tramadol injections from Sri Lakshmi Srinivasa Medical Agency after it failed to produce valid sale records. Another firm, D Roopa Peddinti Medical Agency, purchased five thousand two hundred Tramec injections over two years using fraudulent billing to hide unauthorized supplies. Drug Control Administration Director General Arun Babu stated that licences of those involved would be cancelled and strict legal action taken. These inspections matter because they expose widespread illegal sales and unauthorized storage of controlled painkillers that carry intoxicating effects.
