═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Researchers from the Dinopolis Foundation announced they found the first skeletal evidence of the Diplodocus dinosaur outside North America. The fossils, including fourteen caudal vertebrae and several chevron bones dating back around 150 million years, were uncovered at the La Tejeria site in eastern Spain. The study published this week concluded that the find represents one of the most complete diplodocid skeletons from Europe. Researchers stated that systematic analysis reveals the remains can be confidently referred to as Diplodocus. The authors wrote that this discovery constitutes the first evidence of Diplodocus outside North America and serves as one of the strongest pieces of evidence supporting episodic dispersal events and faunal exchanges across the proto-North Atlantic Ocean during the late Kimmeridgian and early Tithonian period when separating continents were connected by temporary crossing means.
