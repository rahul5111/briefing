═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Quarterback Arch Manning led fourth-ranked Texas back from a 20-point fourth-quarter deficit to defeat top-ranked Ohio State 23-24 on Saturday night. According to Sportradar, the comeback is the largest fourth-quarter rally to win against an Associated Press number-one team in history. 

The game started poorly for Texas as Manning committed early turnovers with a fumbled completion and a fluke interception that helped stake Ohio State to a 10-0 lead. The Buckeyes extended their advantage to 23-3 in the third quarter before a sideline hit on Manning sparked the Longhorns' offense. Manning finished the night completing 23 of 37 passes for 196 yards and one touchdown, directing three scoring drives of 12 plays or longer. Hollywood Smothers secured two short touchdown runs, including the game-winner with just 25 seconds remaining. 

Texas coach Steve Sarkisian praised his quarterback's resilience following the win. His grit started it, Sarkisian said, adding that getting through the adversity of last season helped him get through the adversity of tonight. When he comes back, everybody else comes back. That is a guy who has some grit, some resilience. Manning finished the game reflecting on the response of his team, stating it was a weird start and noting he was really proud of the way they responded.
