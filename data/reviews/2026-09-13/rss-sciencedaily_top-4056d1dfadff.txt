═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

On September 13, 2026, researchers at the University of Pennsylvania School of Engineering and Applied Science published a study in Nature Health revealing that artificial intelligence analysis of more than 400,000 Reddit posts across five years uncovered potentially overlooked symptoms reported by users of GLP-1 medications including Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound. 

The study examined posts from nearly 70,000 users of semaglutide and tirzepatide. While about 44 percent of users described known gastrointestinal side effects, the AI also detected unprompted reports of fatigue, body temperature issues such as chills and hot flashes, and reproductive symptoms including menstrual irregularities. Nearly four percent of users who reported side effects described menstrual changes. 

Researchers noted that the findings do not establish causation between the medications and the reported symptoms. However, because these drugs interact with the hypothalamus, which regulates hormones and body temperature, the patterns may serve as valuable leads. Some side effects like nausea are well known, showing the method works, while underreported symptoms came directly from patients. As senior author Sharath Chandra Guntuku stated, the underreported symptoms are leads that clinicians could potentially pay attention to.
