Southern breakfast chain Biscuitville has been named the nation’s best fast-food breakfast for the fourth consecutive year in the 2026 USA Today 10 Best Readers’ Choice Awards, beating out much larger competitors including McDonald’s, which ranked fourth, and Chick-fil-A, which placed fifth. Burger King and Wendy’s followed in sixth and seventh place. 

The family-owned chain, founded in 1966, achieved the top ranking despite its relatively small footprint of 90 restaurants operating exclusively in North Carolina, South Carolina and Virginia. Biscuitville was chosen by readers after being included among 20 nominees in a public voting process that ran for four weeks before the winners were announced on July 15. The chain also placed third in the separate Best Regional Fast Food category. 

Unlike the efficiency-driven models of national competitors, Biscuitville makes its biscuits from scratch every 15 minutes using a three-ingredient family recipe passed down from the founder’s grandmother, hand-cracks its eggs, and avoids using microwaves or partially cooked food. Chief marketing officer Kristie Mitchell told Fox News Digital that the recognition reflects the daily efforts of its employees and scratch-cooking methods. We have no microwaves, there is no instant anything in our kitchens, Mitchell said, adding that the appeal extends to the overall experience. I think it’s bigger than just the biscuit, it’s all the things that go between that biscuit. 

The company relies on locally sourced ingredients and family-owned suppliers for items such as its eggs, chicken and bacon. While the chain has future plans to expand into states including Tennessee and Georgia, its current focus remains on growing within its existing markets.
