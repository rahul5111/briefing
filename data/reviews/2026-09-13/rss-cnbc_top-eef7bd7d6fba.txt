═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Prediction markets are entering the fall season with daily notional trading volume consistently in the billions, driven by the simultaneous convergence of the NFL season and midterm election campaigns. Major platforms and newer entrants are aggressively expanding their offerings and marketing campaigns to capture market share during what industry leaders describe as a supercycle. 

Polymarket debuted a football season marketing campaign on Tuesday featuring LeBron James, Eli Manning, and Derek Jeter, alongside a new U.S. product enabling social trading. Kalshi experienced a record $2.3 billion in volume traded on Saturday, the opening day of college football. Both platforms faced technical issues during the opening weekend, with Polymarket experiencing a U.S. platform outage and Kalshi prematurely resolving a University of Michigan game market before correcting the results. Meanwhile, platforms continue battling state regulators who argue sports-related event contracts constitute gambling subject to state oversight rather than regulation by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

Newer market entrants are also scaling up for the fall. Rothera, a joint venture between Susquehanna International Group and Robinhood, saw volume surge following a summer World Cup launch. Novig launched an NFL marketing campaign featuring Sydney Sweeney, and ProphetX reported that its revenue doubled from June to August as it prepared to introduce midterm election markets alongside its sports contracts. 

Platforms are utilizing the midterm elections to showcase their breadth beyond sports, benefiting from a longer planning runway than the 2024 presidential cycle. Kalshi launched a midterm elections hub in July and is planning an in-person election night event. 

"You have a bit of a supercycle occurring right now within the prediction markets," said JB Mackenzie, Robinhood's general manager for futures and prediction markets.
