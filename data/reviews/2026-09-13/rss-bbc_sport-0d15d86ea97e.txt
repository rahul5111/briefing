═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli won the Spanish Grand Prix at the new Madring circuit after McLaren pole-sitter Lando Norris lost a comfortable lead due to badly timed virtual safety car. Norris had already passed the pit exit when the VSC was called on lap 15 following Lance Stroll breaking down. That allowed Antonelli and Red Bull second-place finisher Max Verstappen to pit and save time, while the caution period ended before Norris could make it around again. A subsequent slow seven-second pit stop by McLaren dropped Norris down to fifth, and he could only recover to finish third on a track where overtaking proved nearly impossible. Antonelli admitted his eighth win in 14 races this year felt bittersweet, stating it is good to win but it does not feel as good because Lando deserved to win. Norris took the setback in stride, noting they will take it on the chin and that there could be more wins this year. The victory extends Antonelli's championship lead to 81 points over teammate George Russell, who finished fifth on a confusing strategy, and 101 points clear of Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, who retired early with brake failure. Ferrari's Charles Leclerc finished fourth after leading late on an alternative strategy, while Liam Lawson took sixth for Red Bull. Franco Colapinto finished seventh for Alpine, Oscar Piastri eighth for McLaren, Arvid Lindblad ninth for Racing Bulls, and Nico Hulkenberg rounded out the top ten in tenth for Audi.
