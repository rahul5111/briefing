═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI has launched GPT-Live-1 in the API, making its natural voice model available to developers for building voice applications and business workflows. The model listens and speaks simultaneously, handles interruptions instantly, and pairs with models and tools like GPT-6 Astra, Codex, and ChatGPT Work. Unlike traditional cascaded voice agents, GPT-Live-1 processes incoming and outgoing audio together to eliminate latency and preserve conversational rhythm. Developers can control tone, pace, and style through system prompts, and use features including native turn detection, keyword biasing, and alphanumeric recognition. The API costs $0.05 per minute, with backend models and agent harnesses charged separately, and launches with 12 voices spanning multiple accents, dialects, and languages. Early adopters include Yelp Host, Speak, Intercom's Fin, and Cognition's Devin. Language learning app Speak reported nearly 80 percent fewer interruptions compared to previous turn-based systems, while OpenAI stated that GPT-Live-1 scored 30 percentage points higher than GPT-Realtime-2.1 on Full Duplex Bench and ranked first on Tau3 when paired with GPT-6 Astra at medium reasoning effort. One customer reported that adopting the model cut its voice codebase by 80 percent and removed 23,000 lines previously used for real-time patient conversations. Telephony applications target reservations, order updates, and customer service, with enterprise users directed to Presence for workflows that integrate company systems and escalate to humans.
