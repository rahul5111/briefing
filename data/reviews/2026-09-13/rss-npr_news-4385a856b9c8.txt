═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Wisconsin Republican congressman Tom Tiffany and pilot Leonard Boltz survived an emergency plane landing in Lake Wausau on Saturday evening after their single-engine aircraft lost power as it approached Wausau Downtown Airport. Tiffany was returning home from the La Crosse County Lincoln Day Dinner in Onalaska when the mechanical failure occurred. According to the Marathon County Sheriff's Office, one of the occupants called 911 while standing on top of the aircraft before it began to submerge. Tiffany and Boltz exited the plane, spent about 20 minutes in the water and swam to a shallow area to wait for emergency responders who reached them by boat. Tiffany wrote in a post on X that as the plane began to submerge, they exited the aircraft and swam to a shallow area, where they waited until emergency responders reached them by boat. Tiffany received stitches above his right eye and was released from the hospital, while Boltz was treated for minor injuries. The Federal Aviation Administration and the National Transportation Safety Board will investigate the incident.
