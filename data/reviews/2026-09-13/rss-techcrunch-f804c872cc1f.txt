═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Automattic has confirmed that WordPress founder Matt Mullenweg has returned as CEO following an attempted ouster by the company board. An Automattic spokesperson stated via email to TechCrunch just after 5 PM ET on Saturday that Mullenweg holds the position of chairman and CEO with the full support of the board and top executives. Earlier in the week, the board had voted to place Mullenweg on a paid leave of absence, temporarily installing Chief Financial Officer Mark Davies as interim CEO. Mullenweg resisted the move by removing other admins from the company Slack, telling employees he was back in control, and posting messages declaring he was a pirate. During the turmoil, board member and founding CEO Toni Schneider reportedly stepped down, though neither Schneider nor Automattic immediately responded to requests for comment regarding changes to the board composition.
