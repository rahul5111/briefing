═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Leader of the Opposition in Bihar Tejashwi Yadav on September 13, 2026, criticized the State and central governments over their handling of recent floods, claiming Bihar is being treated like a stepchild. Mr. Yadav stated that over 48.35 lakh people across 87 blocks in 15 districts have been affected, according to the Bihar Disaster Management Department. The Rashtriya Janata Dal National Working President said he wrote to Prime Minister Narendra Modi demanding the floods be declared a national disaster, along with compensation and a special economic package, but claimed the central government has shown no seriousness. He also wrote to Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary without result, alleging that the double-engine government diverted four thousand crore rupees from the Contingency Fund in July for salaries and administrative expenses, leaving the treasury empty. Mr. Yadav contrasted the current response with the 2008 Kosi floods, when his father, then Union Minister for Railways Lalu Prasad, secured a special package while Prime Minister Manmohan Singh and Congress President Sonia Gandhi visited the state. He alleged that despite Bihar having eight NDA ministers and 30 Lok Sabha MPs, the state is ignored during disasters only for leaders to seek votes during elections. Describing his own visit to affected areas, Mr. Yadav claimed the State Disaster Response Force boat provided to him began to deflate mid-river, requiring villagers to rescue him.
