═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day for September 13, 2026, features a time-lapse video of Comet C/2020 F3, known as Comet NEOWISE, rising over the Adriatic Sea from Italy. Captured by photographer Paolo Girotti before sunrise on July 8, 2020, the video combines more than 240 individual images taken over a span of 30 minutes. The footage shows the inner Solar System intruder ascending through a foreground of undulating noctilucent clouds against a background of distant stars. Comet NEOWISE was one of the few comets visible to the unaided eye this century and remained unexpectedly bright for over a month, with ion and dust tails emanating from a nucleus approximately five kilometers across.
