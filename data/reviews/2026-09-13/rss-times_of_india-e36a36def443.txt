═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Prime Minister Narendra Modi warned against the weaponisation of technology and critical minerals during the final session of the Brics summit, attended by Chinese President Xi Jinping. Modi addressed leaders of the grouping and partner countries on the four pillars of India’s Brics chairship: resilience, innovation, cooperation, and sustainability. His remarks reflected global concerns regarding China’s near monopoly over critical mineral processing and Western tendencies to use technology for political exclusion. The New Delhi Declaration, issued on Saturday, emphasized the promotion of reliable, diversified, and just supply chains for critical minerals to ensure benefit sharing and value addition in resource-rich nations. Modi stated that the weaponisation of technology and critical minerals can hinder shared progress and stressed the need for inclusive technology adoption tailored to the Global South. Highlighting resilience in the face of global conflicts, pandemics, climate disasters, and supply chain disruptions, Modi pointed to new initiatives such as the Integrated Early Warning System and the Logistics Supply Chain Cooperation Framework. Under innovation, he introduced the Brics Incubator Network, the Brics Startup Innovation Fund, and the Brics Network on Digital Agriculture to connect artificial intelligence, geospatial technology, and Digital Public Infrastructure to farmers. To foster cooperation, Modi noted the launch of the Brics CONNECT program and the Brics MSME Cooperation Portal aimed at small enterprises. For sustainability, he announced the establishment of the Brics Digital Centre of Excellence for Smart Grids and Energy Storage to enhance clean energy systems. Modi remarked that the confidence of Global South countries in Brics has grown because their voices are heard and solutions are developed together with them.
