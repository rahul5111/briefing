═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Demand for OpenAI’s newest and most powerful model, Astra, has forced the company to temporarily pause new sign-ups for its two-hundred-dollar-per-month Pro subscription plan due to infrastructure strain. Thibault Sottiaux, OpenAI’s product leader for core products like Codex and ChatGPT, announced the move on X, explaining that the top-tier plan places the heaviest burden on company systems. Sottiaux wrote that they wanted to take the smallest step that allows them to continue giving the broadest access possible. The company's other offerings, including the API and lower-cost Go and Plus plans, remain available to users. OpenAI previously warned of the potential restriction on Wednesday, when Sottiaux described demand for the model as unprecedented and unlike anything the company had seen during its previous steep periods of growth. Launched on September third, Astra has been rolling out across Pro, Plus, Enterprise, and Business accounts, and was heralded by OpenAI as the beginning of the artificial general intelligence era due to its generational leap in AI reasoning, coding, and computer use. OpenAI has not yet disclosed how long the Pro tier sign-ups will remain disabled or specific daily subscriber numbers to quantify the scale of the surge.
