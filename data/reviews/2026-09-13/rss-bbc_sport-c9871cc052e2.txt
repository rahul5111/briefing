═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Premier League refereeing body Pro Ref has admitted an error of judgement in awarding Erling Haaland's 60th-minute winner during Manchester City's derby victory against Manchester United at Old Trafford. Haaland turned home Josko Gvardiol's cross at the back post, which was initially disallowed for offside on the field. The video assistant referee, Matt Donohue, intervened and awarded the goal after ruling Haaland was played onside by Patrick Dorgu's boot. However, Pro Ref acknowledged that the VAR failed to recognise the likely impact of offside player Enzo Fernandez, who attempted a diving header in the six-yard box, and should have recommended an on-field review. Contact was made with Manchester United to acknowledge the error, and a review of the incident will take place. United defender Lisandro Martinez called the decision an injustice, while manager Michael Carrick said he was confused by the interpretation. Earlier in the match, on 23 minutes, City's Phil Foden was shown a straight red card by referee Michael Oliver for kicking out at United captain Bruno Fernandes while on the turf, a decision confirmed after a VAR check.
