═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A Russian drone struck a train engine near Yahodyn, two kilometers from the Poland border, shortly after former UK Prime Minister Boris Johnson and other European security officials passed through the area on a separate train. No casualties were reported because passengers had been evacuated just fifteen minutes before the strike. The officials, including UK national security adviser Jonathan Powell and former CIA chief David Petraeus, were returning from the Yalta European Strategy conference in Kyiv. Ukraine's state railway company Ukrzaliznytsia stated that the diplomatic train carrying the officials may have been the intended target, noting it had departed early due to timetable changes prompted by strike threats. Former Swedish Prime Minister Carl Bildt, who also attended the conference, reported that passengers on his train were ordered to prepare for evacuation before being allowed to proceed safely to Poland's border station in Dorohusk. Writing on X, Boris Johnson called the action a random and senseless attack and urged allies to urgently provide Ukraine with necessary air defenses. Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha condemned the strikes as Putin's terror knocking directly on the doors of the EU and NATO, while Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk held an emergency meeting to discuss the incident, stating that Russian actions are getting closer to the Polish border. The strike comes amid an escalation of Russian attacks targeting Ukraine's transportation network, which has severely damaged the country's primary mode of transport since air travel was suspended following the start of the full-scale invasion in 2022.
