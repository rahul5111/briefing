═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Turkish police arrested at least 162 people and searched the offices of six LGBTQ+ associations during weekend raids across 15 provinces. Justice Minister Akin Gurlek stated that the operations involved judicial proceedings against 162 suspects, nine associations, and 13 businesses, and were coordinated by prosecutors in Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir, Aydin, and Mersin. Authorities reported seizing drugs, digital equipment, smuggled alcohol, and a gun during operations targeting gay bars, night clubs, and a hammam. The Ankara-based rights group Kaos GL claimed the government also restricted access to dozens of websites and social media accounts belonging to associate groups and advocates. The operations were part of President Recep Tayyip Erdogan's Decade of Family and Population scheme, dubbed My Family is Safe, aimed at reversing declining birthrates. Justice Minister Gurlek stated in a social media post, As per the duty to protect families and children given to the state by the constitution, no criminal organization, abuse network or illegal activity which targets children, youths or families will be tolerated. The Istanbul prosecutor's office reported finding evidence that minors were being inappropriately swayed on sexual orientation and gender identity, while rights groups asserted the government is unfairly targeting the LGBTQ+ community.
