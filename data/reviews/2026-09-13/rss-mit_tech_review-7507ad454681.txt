═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

In October 2021, Eli and Kaitlyn Regalado launched a new cryptocurrency called INDXcoin after believing they received divine instructions to sell digital currency to fellow Christians. Promoting the coin through family, friends, and evangelical contacts, the couple convinced more than 500 people to hand over a total of more than $3 million. Among them were retired pharmacy technician Debbie Bonilla and her husband, Jose, who withdrew $70,000 from their retirement accounts starting in November 2022. Within a year, the project collapsed and investors lost everything, with Debbie describing how the money simply evaporated. In January 2024, the Colorado Division of Securities filed a civil case accusing the Regalados of using their Christian faith to dupe investors into buying worthless crypto, alleging the couple spent roughly $1.3 million, or nearly 40 percent of the raised funds, on personal expenses including vacations, designer clothing, jewelry, a Range Rover, and extensive home renovations. In July 2025, Denver’s district attorney charged the couple with 40 felonies, including theft, racketeering, and securities fraud. The Regalados maintain they did nothing wrong and were simply following God's wishes, with Eli stating, If you think following the Lord is reckless, then yeah, we were very reckless.
