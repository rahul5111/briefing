═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Astronomy Picture of the Day for September 11, 2026, features the spiral galaxy M83, also known as the Southern Pinwheel or the Thousand-Ruby Galaxy, captured in a sharp ground-based telescopic view by Aldo Zanetti. Located approximately twelve million light-years away near the southeastern tip of the constellation Hydra, M83 spans 40,000 light-years across, making it smaller than the Milky Way. The galaxy's spiral arms feature prominent dark dust lanes, blue star clusters, and reddish star-forming regions. Its core shines brightly at x-ray energies, revealing a high concentration of neutron stars and black holes resulting from an intense burst of star formation. M83 belongs to a galaxy group that also includes the active galaxy Centaurus A.
