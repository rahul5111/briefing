═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Canada offered support on Thursday to boost Ukraine’s air defenses and help replenish its drone stocks during a visit by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to Calgary, Alberta, where Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney hosted him. The leaders signed an agreement to negotiate a hundred-year partnership covering defense, critical minerals, and energy. Carney assured Zelenskyy that Canada will always be with you, stating that Ukrainians helped build Canada and Canadians will help rebuild Ukraine. The announced support includes C$350m, equivalent to US$253m, provided through the United States Jumpstart program. Carney also announced C$450m in loan guarantees to enhance the Ukrainian energy supply by repairing damaged infrastructure, increasing storage capacity, and improving power to hospitals to help Ukrainians get through the winter. Additionally, Canada announced a deal to send 30 percent of its new drone production to Ukraine. Zelenskyy stated that these drones will go to Ukraine immediately to the battlefield and to soldiers to save families first of all. Addressing potential negotiations, Zelenskyy said he did not anticipate substantive talks during Russia’s ongoing election period, but planned to meet Donald Trump in New York City in late September, likely on the sidelines of the UN general assembly, as Washington bids to revive long-stalled talks to end the war. Following the Calgary meeting, Zelenskyy was scheduled to visit Banff to address a cabinet retreat for Carney’s liberal government, before traveling to Toronto and North Bay.
