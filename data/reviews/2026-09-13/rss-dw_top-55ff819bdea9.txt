═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Germany's Goethe-Institut is closing all of its Russian branches by Sunday, September 13, 2026. The Russian Foreign Ministry ordered the closure of the German cultural centers in Moscow, St. Petersburg, and Novosibirsk in retaliation for Berlin's actions following an attempted drone attack at Leipzig airport in early August. In early September, German authorities blamed Moscow for the attack, announcing sanctions that included closing the Russian House culture and education center in Berlin, shutting the Russian Consulate General in Bonn, and tightening visa restrictions for Russian citizens. Goethe-Institut Secretary General Gitte Zschoch stated that all employees must cease their work by September 13, ending book loans, teacher training, and access to German language and culture programs. In 2025, approximately 10,000 people studied German through the institute's programs and partnerships, down from about 16,000 before 2023. The institute also administered roughly 22,000 exams and recorded 6,700 library visitors last year. Goethe-Institut President Gesche Joost called the closures the end of an era in German-Russian cultural relations.
