═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board has clarified that there is no proposal to remove any meter readers currently working with the board. HMWS&SB managing director K. Ashok Reddy stated that a special meeting would be held on Tuesday to deliberate on concerns raised by meter readers and to disclose decisions taken. The clarification follows a fresh demand for regularisation by meter readers after the deaths of three outsourcing employees. The board previously sent proposals to the government regarding regularisation, and in November 2025 wrote to the Finance Department seeking clarification on whether meter readers could be included in the Integrated Financial Management and Information System portal, noting that billing and collection agency work is classified as activity outsourcing. According to an April 2026 letter to the MA&UD Engineering department, the proposed strength of meter readers stands at 782 against a current working strength of 677. These workers are engaged through manpower agencies via open tenders and are paid ₹15,600 per month, excluding statutory social security benefits. Further clarification requests were sent to the MA&UD department in February 2026. Addressing pending salaries, the board stated that delays occurred because agencies submitted attendance details late, requiring additional time for bill scrutiny and clearance, with payments now being released based on eligibility. Additionally, the board confirmed that the tender process for engaging manpower agencies has been stopped pending government clarification.
