═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google is launching the Fairwind Program to provide a trusted group of Google Cloud customers, government agencies, and cybersecurity partners with access to advanced AI tools designed to proactively solve cyber risks at scale. The program currently has more than 650 participating partners globally. As an initial step, the program grants defenders access to the Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber model paired with the CodeMender harness. This combination is designed to give defenders the specialized reasoning needed to autonomously find, verify, and write deployment-ready code fixes within a secure cloud environment in minutes, operating at a fraction of the cost of traditional frontier models. Initial access is being staged for organizations critical to societal resilience, including governments and national cyber authorities, critical infrastructure operators across healthcare, telecommunications, energy, and financial networks, and core technology platforms. Participating organizations must agree to strict operational standards, such as restricting access to internal cybersecurity, incident response, or penetration testing teams and deploying multi-factor authentication. Additionally, Google.org released its 2026 US Cybersecurity Impact Report, detailing $36 million in funding for 35 cyber clinics that have provided free security support to over 1,250 hospitals, public school districts, and municipal utilities in the United States, bringing Google's total global cybersecurity funding to more than $100 million.
