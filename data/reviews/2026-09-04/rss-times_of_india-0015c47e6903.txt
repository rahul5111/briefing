═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

US President Donald Trump has issued a trade ultimatum demanding that the Federal Reserve lower interest rates or face a halt to trade with countries running a trade surplus with the United States. Trump posted the threat on Truth Social on Friday following a jobs report showing US employers added 162,000 workers in August, with the unemployment rate holding at 4.1%. Writing that a strong country means a lower interest rate, Trump demanded that the Federal Reserve institute the lowest rate of any country in the world and exhorted the Fed board, led by Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh, to be patriots and stop putting the US at an unfair disadvantage. Warsh, however, has emphasized that the central bank's focus remains on bringing inflation back to its fixed two percent target, leaving the Fed with little reason to cut rates based on the robust labor market. The threat carries significant implications for India, with which the US ran a 58.4 billion dollar goods deficit while total two-way goods trade reached about 149 billion dollars. Through the first seven months of 2026, the US goods deficit with India reached 28.4 billion dollars. Implementation of Trump's threat would leave Indian exporters facing the loss of access to their largest market, while US companies and consumers would be forced to seek alternative suppliers for goods including textiles, pharmaceuticals, machinery, and electronics.
