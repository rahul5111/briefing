═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Communist Party of India Marxist leader P. Jayarajan has alleged that Sree Krishna Jayanti processions in Keralam are part of an RSS political project designed to expand the organization’s influence among families in the State. Speaking on a recent podcast series hosted by the CPI(M) Kannur district committee, Mr. Jayarajan stated that processions marking Chathaya Dinam, the birth anniversary of social reformer Sree Narayana Guru, were historically the only such processions seen on the streets of Keralam. He claimed that Sree Krishna Jayanti celebrations were introduced later as part of efforts by the RSS to build a political base. Mr. Jayarajan noted that while faith in Lord Krishna has a long history in the region, lower-caste individuals were historically barred from entering Sree Krishna temples until the Temple Entry Proclamation, and even members of the Nair community faced restrictions. Faith in Lord Krishna has existed in Keralam for a long time, but people belonging to the lower castes were not allowed entry into Sree Krishna temples. This continued until the Temple Entry Proclamation, Mr. Jayarajan said, adding that even members of the Nair community faced restrictions on temple entry, as pointed out by social reformer Mannath Padmanabhan. It is within this historical context of restricted access that the Sree Krishna Jayanti processions should be viewed as a political project, he maintained. The remarks were published on September 5, 2026.
