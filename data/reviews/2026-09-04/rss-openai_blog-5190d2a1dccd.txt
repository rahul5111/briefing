═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI is launching a one billion dollar global initiative called Daybreak for Frontline Defenders to provide subsidized access to frontier AI cyber models, training, technical support, and partnerships. The funding is targeted for consumption over the next six months, prioritizing resource-constrained operators of essential services in the United States and internationally, including water and wastewater systems, electric grid operators, state and local governments, community and regional banks, nonprofits, and open-source maintainers. The initiative includes Daybreak for America, which features a new pilot with the Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center to train and support state, local, tribal, and territorial cyber defenders. Additionally, the Daybreak Defense Network is rolling out more than 35 enterprise products and partner-operated services to integrate the cyber models into workflows defenders already use. The effort builds on earlier assistance following recent attacks on United States water systems, where OpenAI offered affected states and utilities up to one million dollars in no-cost application programming interface credits, Daybreak access, and technical assistance. Daybreak offers Daybreak Blue for common defensive work and Daybreak Red for specialized cyber work, with thousands of defenders across 2,000 approved organizations currently using the platform. OpenAI stated that AI-enabled cyber attacks will become more widespread and sophisticated in the coming months, creating a narrowing defender window. As OpenAI noted, no single company can secure the systems we all depend on, and every organization has a role to play.
