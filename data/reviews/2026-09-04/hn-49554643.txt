═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI is introducing GPT-6 Astra, its newest intelligence and alignment model, which is rolling out to a limited set of organizations today and to all ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, as well as through the OpenAI API, Microsoft Azure, and AWS Bedrock over the coming days.

Astra saturates FrontierMath Tier 4 with a score of 98%, achieves 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3, and scores 100% on ExploitBench. On OSWorld 2.0 latency simulations, Astra achieves a 72.6% computer-use score in roughly 40 minutes per task, operating about 47% faster than the previous GPT-5.6 Sol model, which scored 65.7% at roughly 75 minutes. Combined with an updated Codex harness, task completion is 1.9 times faster than the previous experience on the Mind2Web benchmark.

In cybersecurity evaluations, Astra met the Critical threshold under the company's Preparedness Framework. Without production safeguards, Astra achieved a 100% score on ExploitBench compared to 78.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol, and a 42.4% success rate on ExploitGym compared to 30.3%. On SRE-Bench, Astra solved 88.0% of software binary tasks in a single attempt and 99.2% within four attempts, outperforming GPT-5.6 Sol's 55.9% and 68.7%. During internal testing on a novel exploit development evaluation using vulnerabilities from June to August 2026, Astra discovered and used two previously unknown zero-day vulnerabilities.

Regarding alignment, when tested against a difficult task informed by the Hugging Face incident, GPT-6 Astra went beyond its intended scope in 0% of cases, compared to 48% for GPT-5.6 Sol without production safeguards. The model will refuse advanced cybersecurity tasks such as creating proof-of-concept exploits upon launch, though OpenAI plans to expand access through OpenAI Daybreak in the coming weeks.
