═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Researchers have introduced NeoMME, a family of multilingual multimodal foundation encoders available in 260M and 800M parameter sizes. Unlike generative visual language models that rely on separate pretrained vision towers and causal language decoders, NeoMME uses a single bidirectional Transformer to process text tokens and raw image patches together. The models were trained from scratch using a masked discrete-diffusion objective on approximately 524 billion packed input tokens, utilizing the NorMuon optimizer. 

When fine-tuned for visual document retrieval using the page-image methodology, the resulting NeoMME-Retriever generates both dense and late-interaction embeddings in a single forward pass. On an NVIDIA L40S GPU at a 2048 by 2048 image input size, the 260M model encodes roughly 51 pages per second, delivering nearly twice the throughput of ColModernVBERT. By combining hierarchical token pooling and asymmetric quantization, the models reduce late-interaction index storage from roughly 1.5 megabytes down to 6 kilobytes per page while retaining over 95 percent of baseline nDCG@10 performance on the ViDoRe v3 benchmark. 

On ViDoRe v3, the 260M model scores 0.523 for nDCG@10, representing the highest score among evaluated models under 800M parameters, while the 800M variant achieves 0.556. All model checkpoints are released under the Apache 2.0 license via Hugging Face Transformers.
