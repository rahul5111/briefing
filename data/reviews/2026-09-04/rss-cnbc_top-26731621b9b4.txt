═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Tesla shares slid six percent on Friday following an update on the company's Cybercab robotaxi that failed to impress investors. The drop wiped out a five and a half percent gain from Thursday ahead of the event. The Cybercab is a bronze-colored, two-seat vehicle with butterfly doors and no steering wheel or pedals that has been in production since April. Tesla held an invite-only event in Austin, Texas, on Thursday that was not streamed, and CEO Elon Musk did not make an appearance. The company announced that users of its Tesla Robotaxi ride-hailing app can now catch a driverless trip in a Cybercab within a geofenced area around Austin, though users have already shared videos and complaints regarding routing errors, missed destinations, and excessive wait or ride times. Analysts at RBC Capital Markets wrote in a note on Friday that Tesla offered limited new disclosure, stating that the event lacked a public livestream, which was a notable departure from Tesla's traditionally theatrical product reveals. Wells Fargo analysts published a note titled TSLA Cybercab Launch Event Underwhelms, noting that the Austin service is facing early execution issues. Additionally, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration initiated an audit query on Thursday to determine if Tesla properly self-certified the Cybercab as safe for public roads and compliant with federal safety standards.
