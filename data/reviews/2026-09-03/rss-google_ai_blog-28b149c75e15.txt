═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google is launching the Fairwind Program to provide a trusted group of Google Cloud customers, government agencies, and cybersecurity partners with access to advanced AI and cyber defense capabilities. The program features Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber combined with the CodeMender harness, designed to help defenders autonomously find, verify, and fix code vulnerabilities in minutes rather than weeks within a secure cloud environment. Initial access is being staged for more than 650 participating partners globally, including governments and national cyber authorities, critical infrastructure operators across healthcare, telecommunications, energy, and financial networks, and core technology platforms. Participating organizations must agree to strict operational standards, such as limiting access to internal cybersecurity teams and deploying multi-factor authentication. Alongside the Fairwind launch, Google released its 2026 Google.org US Cybersecurity Impact Report, detailing $36 million in funding for 35 cyber clinics that provide free security support to over 1,250 U.S. hospitals, public school districts, and municipal utilities, bringing Google's total global cybersecurity funding to more than $100 million.
