═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI is launching a one billion dollar global initiative called Daybreak for Frontline Defenders to provide subsidized access to frontier AI cyber capabilities, training, and technical support. The funding is targeted for consumption over the next six months and focuses on resource constrained operators of essential services, including water and wastewater systems, electric grid operators, state and local governments, community and regional banks, nonprofits, and open-source maintainers. The effort includes Daybreak for America, which features a new pilot with the Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center to train and support state, local, tribal, and territorial cyber defenders. Additionally, the Daybreak Defense Network is introducing more than 35 enterprise products and partner-operated services to integrate the cyber models into existing workflows. The initiative builds on previous support that offered up to one million dollars in no-cost API credits and technical assistance to water systems following recent attacks. OpenAI states that AI-enabled cyber attacks will become more widespread and sophisticated in the coming months, creating a narrowing defender window to close security gaps before attackers exploit them. Eligible organizations can visit the Daybreak website to apply for access and support.
