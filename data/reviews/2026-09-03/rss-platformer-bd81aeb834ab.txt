═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Clara Shih, former CEO of Salesforce AI and former head of Meta's business AI group, has left Meta after being "radicalized" by observing how efficiently AI agents handle complex workplace tasks. Last fall at Meta, Shih watched AI agents compress a product development process involving user researchers, designers, product managers, and three types of engineers down to just one or two people and a prototype. 

Seeing similar efficiency gains in marketing, distribution, and privacy review, Shih began canceling entry-level job postings because she no longer felt they were needed. This spring, she stepped down from her operational role to launch the New Work Foundation, a nonprofit, alongside a consumer brand called Dear CC that provides free tools, a podcast, a data tool called Field Report, and a mentoring app called Game Plan for entry-level workers. 

Reflecting on her previous belief that automation would simply free workers for higher-order tasks, Shih stated that this optimistic narrative has "primarily not been true." Citing research from MIT economist David Autor, Shih outlined how AI reshapes jobs across three distinct cases, noting that one in five corporate roles is especially vulnerable to disruption.
