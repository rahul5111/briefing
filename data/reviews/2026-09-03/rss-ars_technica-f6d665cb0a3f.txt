═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A prominent US senator is asking the National Security Agency to provide public guidance on best practices for using virtual private networks to secure communications against foreign espionage. While US agencies have previously recommended VPNs, which route internet traffic through encrypted connections to remote servers and mask user IP addresses, none have issued recommendations on which services offer adequate protection. Technical limitations often undermine these security assurances, as encrypted tunnels typically terminate once a single server decrypts traffic to forward it to its final destination, potentially exposing decrypted data or IP addresses to hackers or rogue employees. Additionally, VPNs fail to encrypt certain metadata like timestamps, leaving users vulnerable to profiling by nation-states engaged in intelligence gathering.
