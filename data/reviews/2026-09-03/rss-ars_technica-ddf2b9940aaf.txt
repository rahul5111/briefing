The Federal Communications Commission announced plans to create a robocall mitigation scorecard to rate phone companies on their effectiveness in blocking illegal spam calls. The proposed tool would evaluate wireless, wireline, and VoIP providers that have retail customers, excluding those operating solely as wholesale or intermediate providers. Scorecards could grade providers using a number scale, letter grades, or risk classifications such as low, medium, or high risk. The metrics would incorporate call-blocking statistics, customer complaints, enforcement actions, and data on how often legitimate calls are mistakenly blocked. The evaluation aims to move beyond a simple administrative checklist to reflect operational practices and measurable outcomes. As FCC Chairman Brendan Carr stated, combatting illegal robocalls remains the agency's top consumer protection priority. The FCC Consumer and Governmental Affairs Bureau noted that the scorecard will empower consumers and encourage providers to continue combatting illegal robocalls by providing a public assessment of their protection efforts.
