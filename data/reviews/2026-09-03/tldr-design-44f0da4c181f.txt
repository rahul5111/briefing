═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google is rolling out a new image creation and editing tool called Google Pics to all Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and most Workspace business customers over the coming weeks. Built on the Nano Banana image generation and editing model, the tool will function as both a standalone product and an integrated feature within Google Workspace apps, starting with Slides, Docs, and Drive. 

Google Pics includes several fine-tuned editing capabilities designed for precise control. Object segmentation allows users to isolate and transform specific objects using text prompts without altering the rest of the image, while executing multiple edits at once. Users can also modify or translate text directly inside images without disrupting the design or changing the font. The tool generates multiple options from a single prompt and supports real-time collaboration, enabling users to share creations and edit the same image together with teammates or friends. 

By integrating Google Pics directly into existing Workspace applications, the company aims to eliminate the need for users to jump between applications, copy and paste, or switch tabs when creating and sharing visuals.
