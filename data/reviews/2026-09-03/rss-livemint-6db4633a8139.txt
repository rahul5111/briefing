═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

President Donald Trump rejected reports that the United States is running short of ammunition during its five-month war with Iran, calling claims of dwindling supplies one hundred percent wrong and labeling critics spreading them as treasonous. Writing on Truth Social, Trump stated that the country has virtually unlimited amounts of mid- to high-grade ammunition and is producing munitions at levels never seen before. He added that the US temporarily prioritized keeping ammunition for domestic needs rather than foreign sales, which will resume soon, and criticized the previous Biden administration for providing hundreds of billions of dollars in assistance to Ukraine and NATO without requiring European allies to pay for it. Trump's comments responded to reports, including an August 4 Reuters story citing three people familiar with the data, indicating that the US Army has used virtually all of two key surface-to-surface missile systems, the ATACMS and the newer Precision Strike Missile, during the conflict. The depletion of these long-range missiles, which cost more than one million dollars each, has raised concerns among US officials about Pentagon readiness for potential future conflicts with major adversaries like Russia and China. Military leaders have also warned Trump in recent weeks that supplies of defensive weapons, including Patriot missile interceptors, were falling, though a US official disputed claims that stockpile concerns stopped a major offensive, saying instead that pressure from Gulf states influenced the decision. Despite these challenges, a source familiar with the situation noted that Central Command has been able to draw from global military stockpiles to replenish supplies.
