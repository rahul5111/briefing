═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Union Home Secretary Govind Mohan chaired a high-level meeting with Himalayan States and Union Territories on Thursday, September 3, 2026, to review preparedness for Glacial Lake Outburst Floods and snow avalanches. The review follows deadly flash floods in Nepal that killed hundreds and left thousands missing. Chief Secretaries and State Disaster Management Authorities from Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, and Jammu and Kashmir attended alongside senior officials from the Ministry of Home Affairs, the National Disaster Management Authority, the Central Water Commission, the India Meteorological Department, and the Defence Geoinformatics Research Establishment. Participants evaluated vulnerable glacial lakes, high-risk zones, flow path mapping, and early warning dissemination. Mr. Mohan stressed that preparedness efforts must move beyond a response-oriented approach and focus on preventive mitigation, risk reduction, and community-level readiness. The Centre directed states to expedite GLOF mitigation projects sanctioned in 2024, strengthen inter-agency coordination, and ensure continuous surveillance and regular sharing of risk information to minimize potential loss of life and property.
