═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Belkin is entering the semi-solid-state power bank market, joining companies like BMX, Kuxiu, and Zens with the launch of its first accessories featuring this battery technology. The new power banks utilize what Belkin calls BoostSolid Cell technology, which incorporates a gel-like layer alongside the traditional liquid electrolyte found in standard lithium-ion batteries. According to the company, these accessories have been engineered to last up to 3x longer than other power banks. Additional advantages of the technology include a reduced risk of overheating that could potentially cause swelling or fires, as well as consistent power delivery across a wider temperature range. While the new devices offer these durability and safety improvements, they are more expensive than comparable power banks with similar specifications.
