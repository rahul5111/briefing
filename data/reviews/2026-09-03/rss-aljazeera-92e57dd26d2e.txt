═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Federal prosecutors have charged Immigration and Customs Enforcement officer Christian Castro with lying under oath to investigators regarding the January shooting of 24-year-old Julio Cesar Sosa-Celis during an immigration crackdown in Minnesota. Prosecutors alleged that Castro lied about the events leading up to him firing through the door of a Minnesota home, hitting Sosa-Celis in the leg. Castro, a former Department of Homeland Security agent, also faces multiple state felony charges in Minnesota related to the nonfatal shooting, including assault with a dangerous weapon. Minnesota authorities had sought to extradite Castro from Texas, but Texas Governor Greg Abbott declined to cooperate, leading to Castro being detained in Texas for 90 days before his release under state law. The federal indictment follows the emergence of surveillance video that contradicted Castro's account that Sosa-Celis had attacked him. Todd Lyons, then acting head of ICE, stated at the time that the footage revealed sworn testimony provided by separate officers appeared to contain untruthful statements. The incident began after Castro and a fellow agent chased Venezuelan national Alfredo Aljorna following a traffic stop to a home shared with Sosa-Celis. Castro then shot through the closed door, knowing the home was occupied by several people, which included two children according to a witness. Federal authorities have suspended Castro and dropped initial charges against Sosa-Celis and his co-defendant, while the other agent involved has been suspended but not yet charged.
