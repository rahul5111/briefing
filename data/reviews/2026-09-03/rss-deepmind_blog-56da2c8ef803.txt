═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google DeepMind and Google Research have introduced WeatherNext 3, a global weather AI model that generates hourly forecasts at higher resolutions and improved accuracy compared to previous versions. According to independent live evaluations by Brightband, it is the most advanced and accurate global weather model to date.

WeatherNext 3 produces surface variables like temperature and moisture at a 5-kilometer resolution, other surface variables at 10 kilometers, and atmospheric variables like wind speed at 25 kilometers. This provides a global weather picture roughly five times sharper than its predecessor, WeatherNext 2, which used a 25-kilometer grid in 6-hour increments. 

Unlike models trained on complex physics simulations that carry a six-hour data lag, WeatherNext 3 learns directly from real-time observations by ingesting live global geostationary satellite data and sparse weather station observation data. This enables the model to update hourly and bypass traditional modeling constraints. For precipitation forecasting, the model shows a Continuous Ranked Probability Score improvement of up to 60 percent against NASA’s IMERG, 30 percent for MRMS, and 10 percent against rain gauge measurements for early lead times. 

The model also features predictions engineered for renewable energy production, including 100-meter wind speeds for wind-energy output, alongside high-resolution cloud cover and sun radiation levels for solar farms. 

Starting today, WeatherNext 3 is powering weather experiences across Google Search, the Gemini app, Google Maps, the Google Maps Platform Weather API, and Google Earth Engine. Users will see up to 50 percent more accurate precipitation forecasts when planning a day or more ahead. The forecast data is also available to query in BigQuery and Earth Engine, or for bulk-download from Google Cloud Storage.
