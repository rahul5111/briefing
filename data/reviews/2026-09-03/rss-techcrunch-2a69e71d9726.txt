═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Builders Stage will return to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 at the Moscone Center in San Francisco from October 13 to 15, bringing together more than 10,000 founders, investors, startup operators, and technology leaders. As one of six industry-focused stages at the event, the Builders Stage is dedicated to helping founders navigate scaling challenges such as raising capital, hiring talent, building go-to-market engines, and preparing for the transition from seed to Series A. 

The agenda features conversations and case studies led by prominent tech and venture figures, including Grant Lee, CEO and co-founder of Gamma; Leah Solivan, founder and general partner at Precedent.vc; and Robby Stein, VP of Product at Google. Sessions will address practical strategies across 18 distinct panels and fireside chats. Topics range from competing when not building AI and handling competition from foundational model providers like OpenAI, to navigating AI talent compensation, securing pre-seed capital without a product, and shifting product decision-making from an MVP to a billion-user scale. 

Additional sessions will cover early-stage M&A as a competitive strategy, defining fundable metrics for the 2027 Series A fundraising cycle, compressing go-to-market timelines to reach zero to 10 million dollars in annual recurring revenue rapidly, multi-model AI architectures, and spotting false product-market fit. The program will also unveil Greenfield Partners’ 2026 AI Disruptors 60 list and address founder mental health, repeatable multi-product growth, and zero-to-one customer acquisition without a marketing budget. 

Attendees can register and save up to 330 dollars before upcoming ticket price increases.
