═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Astronomy Picture of the Day for September 7, 2026, features the Pelican Nebula, also designated as IC 5070. Captured by photographer Mark Killion from Utah using 25 hours of exposure time, the deep image reveals a busy starfield highlighted by red and blue glowing gas, dark filamentary dust, and dense tentacles of cold gas. The Pelican Nebula is separated from the larger North America Nebula by a molecular cloud filled with dark dust, and it is bounded by the dark nebula LDN 935. The region is an active site of star formation where light from young, energetic stars is actively transforming cold gas into hot gas, creating an advancing ionization front visible in bright orange on the upper right. Millions of years from now, the balance and placement of stars and gas will change the region so that it no longer resembles a pelican. Additionally, the main site for the Astronomy Picture of the Day is moving from apod.nasa.gov to science.nasa.gov/apod.
