═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Australian corporate law firm Gilbert + Tobin has achieved an 87 percent active usage rate among enabled ChatGPT Enterprise users as of June 2026, more than twice the adoption rate the firm typically sees for other software tools. 

The rollout, which began with operations teams before expanding into marketing, finance, recruitment, technology, and parts of the legal practice, focused on improving internal operational workflows rather than delivering legal advice. CEO Sam Nickless introduced the technology to employees with the message, “AI is not cheating,” positioning it to support professional judgment rather than replace it. The firm paired visible leadership support with role-specific enablement, where the business transformation team demonstrated tailored workflows without mandating usage targets.

To ensure security for confidential client and business information, the firm implemented clear usage guidance, role-based access, administrative controls, and an OpenAI environment featuring Australian data residency. 

The technology has reduced processing times across multiple departments. A recruitment research and data extraction workflow dropped from approximately four hours to 20 minutes, while selected conflict, KYC, and AML checks fell from three to eight hours down to five minutes. Additionally, preparing audit reports across 300 entities avoided a full day of manual work. 

Using Codex for multistep operational tasks, the firm also checked and renamed 1,100 files, built a monitoring watchtower for its AWS environment, and developed a custom GPT digital twin of the CEO to help executives pressure-test ideas. 

As CEO Sam Nickless stated, “OpenAI approached the platform from an enterprise perspective and built the controls that organisations like ours need to operate with confidence.”
