═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A journalist used a free AI chatbot to generate a functional mathematical solution for a six-player, six-dice game in about 13 minutes after two clarifying prompts. The problem of finding dice that ensure fair, tie-free throws had occupied a team of human mathematicians for more than a decade. The author sent the AI-derived solution to Eric Harshbarger at Auburn University, Alabama, who confirmed the numbers were correct, though the resulting dice design featured impractical specifications such as five 720-sided dice and one 20-sided die. Harshbarger and his collaborators had previously adapted their own five-dice solution into a six-dice version using 360-sided dice. A search by Google software engineer Paul Meyer showed no trace of the specific solution online, leading the researchers to deduce that the AI likely located existing published descriptions of the five-dice work and an induction method, then filled in the gaps. Commenting on the capability, Harshbarger said, I think it is a big deal. I hate to say there is intelligence there, but it may have figured something out. It is amazing, a little scary, in some ways, but it is amazing. Sébastien Bubeck at OpenAI stated that he expects the progress seen in mathematics over the past six months to be matched in the next six months, though current models are still limited to solving existing problems rather than creating entirely new mathematical concepts.
