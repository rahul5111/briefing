═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

As the new Palestinian school year opened in the occupied West Bank village of al-Mughayyir, head-teacher Bassam al-Assaf and parents faced mounting safety concerns following a surge in settler-related violence. Six people, including three students, have been killed in the village this year. Among them was 14-year-old Aws al-Naasan, who was shot dead outside the school gates in April by a reservist, according to the Israeli military, after stones were thrown at a car. Over the summer, two more pupils and a 19-year-old were shot dead by Israeli forces during settler attacks and incursions. In response to the growing threat, the school has installed barbed-wire fencing, reinforced gates, and stationed ambulance crews outside, while parents volunteer to monitor the grounds.

A UN Commission of Inquiry reported in June that settler attacks are part of a broader coercive strategy to drive Palestinians off their land by intentionally targeting children. Israel dismissed the report as a libellous sham. The regional settlers council stated that it regrets any harm to innocent people and opposes unlawful violence, while rejecting claims of a deliberate plan to harm children or drive residents from their homes. UN figures indicate that injuries to Palestinians from settler and military attacks have risen to an average of three per day this year. Between the beginning of 2025 and the end of July 2026, the UN recorded 316 Palestinians killed and more than 5,600 injured, alongside 20 Israelis killed and 123 injured. All Israeli settlements in the West Bank are illegal under international law, and many surrounding outposts are illegal under Israeli law.
