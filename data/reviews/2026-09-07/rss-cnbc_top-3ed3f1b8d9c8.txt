═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

An Amazon cargo jet operated by 21 Air LLC crashed at Miami International Airport on Sunday at around 2 p.m. after arriving from San Juan, Puerto Rico, overrunning the runway by about 1,300 feet. The accident killed at least five people and injured at least five others. National Transportation Safety Board Chair Jennifer Homendy described the accident scene during a Monday press briefing as utter devastation, stating, "There's debris everywhere." The fatalities occurred when the aircraft struck two vehicles: a 2021 white Ford Econoline van carrying seven people, and a Toyota Corolla Cross located outside the airport boundary after the plane busted through a perimeter fence. The jet also slid through a fence separating an area where Tesla robotaxis were stationed before finally stopping. Investigators have recovered the flight data recorder and cockpit voice recorder, which are headed to NTSB headquarters for evaluation. The NTSB will examine the operational relationship and safety provisions between Amazon and 21 Air. The crash closed two of Miami International Airport's four runways, disrupting holiday travel with more than 160 flight cancellations and nearly 325 delays by late Sunday, with delays continuing into Monday.
