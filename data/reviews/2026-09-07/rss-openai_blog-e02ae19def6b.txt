═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

On Monday 7th September 2026, WAN-IFRA, the Association of Independent Regional Press Publishers of Ukraine, and OpenAI jointly announced a new initiative to support Ukraine’s independent news sector through artificial intelligence adoption, innovation, and resilience. Developed by AIRPPU in partnership with WAN-IFRA and OpenAI, the programme is designed to help Ukrainian news organisations strengthen their sustainability, efficiency, and operational capacity during continued conflict and unrest. The initiative features two main components: Newsroom AI, which focuses on developing AI-driven newsroom projects, and Business Transformation, which targets commercial and operational improvements. The effort includes the Newsroom AI Masterclass Series, which began on August 5, 2026, and brings together international experts to cover editorial workflows, audience engagement, product development, revenue generation, organisational change, and responsible AI adoption. It also includes the Newsroom AI Catalyst, set to launch on September 17, 2026, which will provide hands-on support, implementation roadmaps, and pilot testing for ten selected Ukrainian news organisations. All participating publishers will receive credits for OpenAI's API to build custom newsroom solutions.
