═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Apple’s upcoming September 9 event is expected to feature the company’s first foldable iPhone, alongside updates to AirPods, HomePods, and the iPhone 18 line. The transition also marks Tim Cook handing off the CEO role to John Ternus. 

The foldable device, widely referred to as the iPhone Ultra, is projected to cost more than $2,000. According to Bloomberg insider Mark Gurman, early testers report a premium feel that fits into a pocket and opens into a wide display. The hardware features a 5.5-inch cover display that expands to 7.8 inches when unfolded, a dual-lens rear camera system with main and ultra-wide lenses, MagSafe support, and a side-mounted Touch ID button instead of Face ID. The device runs iOS and is designed with a less visible screen crease than competing foldables.

Apple is also expected to release the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max models this September, with the standard iPhone 18 predicted to arrive six months later. Additionally, a macOS preview build revealed references to refreshed HomePods and unreleased AirPods equipped with built-in cameras. According to Gurman, the earbud cameras are designed to act as eyes for the Siri digital assistant rather than capturing standard photos or video.
