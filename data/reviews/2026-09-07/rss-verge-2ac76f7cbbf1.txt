═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Boox has announced that its new pocket-sized e-reader, the Picco, is expected to launch in November, following an initial teaser in July. Reporting from the IFA trade show revealed new hardware details about the device, though pricing has not yet been disclosed. 

The Picco features a 3.97-inch touchscreen e-paper display. Writing about the inclusion of a touchscreen, The Verge's Andrew Liszewski called it a "welcome upgrade" over previous models like the Xteink X4 Pro. Unlike competing devices such as the Xteink readers, the Picco does not include magnets on the back for phone attachment. 

For navigation, the device is equipped with physical page-turn buttons that support customizable actions for both short and long presses. Additionally, the e-reader appears to lack onboard storage, relying instead entirely on a microSD card for data management.
