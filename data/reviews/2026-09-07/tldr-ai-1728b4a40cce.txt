Artificial intelligence company Anthropic is expected to begin marketing its initial public offering in mid-October at the earliest, with the listing to be completed days before the U.S. midterm elections in November. The public prospectus, previously anticipated as early as next week, is now expected in late September. The schedule shift pushes back what some investors have said could be a $2 trillion listing, ranking among the largest IPOs ever attempted and serving as a major test of public-market appetite for the artificial intelligence industry. Before making the prospectus public, Anthropic is working to finalize a $15 billion revolving credit facility, followed by meetings between analysts and the company. Banks working with Anthropic on the offering include Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Citi. Anthropic and the banks declined to comment.
