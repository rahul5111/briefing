═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Uttar Pradesh Police on Monday detained senior state Congress leaders including State Congress President Ajay Rai in Lucknow after they attempted to visit Saharanpur over the demolition of a historic mosque within the Saharanpur Collectorate complex. Mr. Rai and other leaders were released in the evening after hours of detention. The Congress condemned the police action as dictatorship, stating the administration is working under BJP government directions to silence the opposition. Addressing the incident, Mr. Rai told The Hindu, Following the brutal action of bulldozing the historic mosque located in the Saharanpur Collectorate premises, I along with other party leaders were going to take stock of the situation there today, but we were forcibly detained by the police as soon as he left the Lucknow party office. This authoritarian action is clear evidence that the BJP government is deeply afraid of the Congress party democratic pushback. In a separate development, a Samajwadi Party delegation consisting of MPs and other leaders submitted a petition in Saharanpur addressed to the President of India seeking action against officers responsible for the demolition of the 119-year-old mosque. Meanwhile, an Archaeological Survey of India team on Monday examined a well discovered during debris removal at the demolition site. Sadar Sub-Divisional Magistrate Subodh Kumar stated that ASI officials measured the depth of the well, carried out photography and videography, and collected samples of soil and brick remnants from inside the structure.
