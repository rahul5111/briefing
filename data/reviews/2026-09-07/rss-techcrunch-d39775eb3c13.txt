═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Telluride Film Festival hosted a surprise, unannounced screening on Sunday night of a new documentary about Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, directed by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim. Festivalgoers entered a 650-seat theater with their phones locked away, knowing only that the film ran nearly three hours and had not been shown anywhere previously. Titled You Can See Everything, the documentary captures Holmes and her husband, Billy Evans, during the 34 days leading up to May 2023, when Holmes reported to federal prison to begin an 11-year sentence for defrauding investors regarding her blood-testing technology. Fielder reportedly lived in the couple's guest house during production, and the film also features an A-list actress who becomes part of the narrative. Holmes's subsequent legal appeals to overturn her conviction and shorten her sentence were rejected in February of last year, after which she filed a clemency petition asking President Trump to commute her sentence, a request that remains pending. A24 has scheduled the film for an October release.
