═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI has introduced new capabilities for ChatGPT for Healthcare, including an electronic health record integration with Epic and a Healthcare Public Data plugin designed to connect authorized patient context and official industry data within a governed workspace. 

The Epic integration allows clinicians to bring authorized patient records into ChatGPT to review history, identify changes, and prepare for appointments, or to embed the tool directly into supported EHR layouts. Meanwhile, the Healthcare Public Data plugin provides structured access to nine official public sources, including PubMed, DailyMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, RxNorm, and CMS Coverage, enabling teams to query specific datasets for research, medication data, and policy information without searching each source separately.

To ensure accuracy and safety, OpenAI partners with hundreds of physicians across 60 countries and 26 medical specialties who have reviewed more than 700,000 model responses. In evaluations across 27 clinical use cases involving 4,363 ratings, physicians rated 99.1% of responses safe. In tests covering nuanced clinical questions using large U.S. healthcare datasets, more than 93% of responses across five connected data sources were rated as having good or better accuracy.

The workspace features enterprise controls such as role-based access, single sign-on, audit logs, and support for HIPAA-compliant workflows under an applicable Business Associate Agreement. Eligible U.S. ChatGPT for Clinicians users can install the public data plugin individually, while the EHR integration requires workspace deployment.
