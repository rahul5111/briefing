═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Artificial general intelligence generally refers to AI that is more capable than the average human at many or most tasks. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has described AGI as the equivalent of a median human you could hire as a co-worker, while OpenAI charter defines it as highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work. Google DeepMind views AGI as AI that is at least as capable as humans at most cognitive tasks.

An AI agent is a tool that uses artificial intelligence technologies to perform a series of tasks on a user's behalf beyond the capabilities of a basic chatbot, such as filing expenses, booking reservations, or writing and maintaining code. API endpoints act as buttons on software that other programs use to build integrations and allow AI agents to control third-party services directly. Chain-of-thought reasoning involves large language models breaking down problems into smaller, intermediate steps to improve the quality of logic or coding outputs, optimized through reinforcement learning. 

Deep learning is a subset of self-improving machine learning utilizing multi-layered artificial neural networks inspired by the human brain, allowing systems to identify data characteristics without human definition, though requiring millions of data points and higher development costs. Diffusion systems generate art, music, and text by adding noise to destroy data structure and then learning a reverse diffusion process to recover the data. Distillation extracts knowledge from a large teacher model to train a smaller, more efficient student model, a method likely used to develop GPT-4 Turbo.

Generative Adversarial Networks, or GANs, use a pair of neural networks where a generator creates output and a discriminator evaluates it to produce realistic data like deepfakes. Hallucination occurs when models generate incorrect information due to training data gaps. Inference is the process of running a trained AI model to make predictions, while large language models are deep neural networks made of billions of parameters that process text by predicting likely patterns. Model Context Protocol, or MCP, is an open standard introduced by Anthropic in 2024 that connects AI models to external tools and files without requiring custom connectors.
