═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The government has reduced the minimum gap for booking domestic LPG cylinder refills from 45 days to 25 days for all consumers, Union Minister of State for Petroleum and Natural Gas Suresh Gopi announced on Monday. The Petroleum Ministry directed state-run oil marketing companies to implement the uniform 25-day interval between refill bookings with immediate effect, applying equally to urban and rural areas. Gopi shared a copy of the ministry letter on Facebook.

Varying booking intervals were initially established following the outbreak of the conflict in the Middle East in February 2026, which led the ministry to prescribe timelines of 25 days in urban areas and 45 days in rural areas via a press release on March 12, 2026, as a temporary demand-management measure to ease supply pressures. 

According to the ministry letter, the restrictions were put in place when supplies came under pressure, but the situation has since improved with a considerable reduction in refill backlogs. The ministry stated, “In view of the present LPG supply position and the considerable reduction in refill backlogs as compared to the situation prevailing at the time of introduction of the demand-management measures, the Ministry has decided to revise the existing refill booking interval and prescribe an inter-refill booking gap of 25 days uniformly for all Domestic LPG consumers, including those in rural areas.”

State-run firms Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited, and Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited were instructed to follow the new rule immediately. Previously, the government had set maximum LPG production targets for 24 refinery and upstream oil companies with a combined production capacity of 63.81 thousand metric tonnes per day to bolster domestic cooking-gas supplies and enhance resilience against potential disruptions.
