═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Circuit diagrams posted to Weibo by user Fried White Rice suggest that the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro models, powered by the anticipated A20 Pro chip, may feature a seven-core GPU and a faster memory interface. The leaked images show where the chip attaches to the underlying circuit board, forming the basis for architectural inferences. The diagrams indicate six CPU cores in the same configuration as the previous model, consisting of two high-performance cores and four efficiency cores. However, the L2 cache for the small CPU cores appears to have increased from 6 MB in the A19 Pro to 8 MB, while the L2 cache for the large CPU cores remains at 16 MB. Regarding the graphics processing unit, analyzing the solder-ball positions and layout across multiple configurations points to a seven-core GPU as the most plausible arrangement, an increase of one core over its predecessor. This follows a recent suggestion by Fixed Focus Digital that the A20 Pro chip will deliver an 18 percent speed increase over the previous model alongside 30 percent better power efficiency.
