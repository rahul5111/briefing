═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Lok Sabha Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi on Monday accused Prime Minister Narendra Modi of using insulting labels like dimagi Naxal to silence and shame those seeking government accountability. Gandhi responded to the phrase while targeting the government over a building collapse incident in Delhi. Prime Minister Modi first used the term dimagi Naxal during his August 15 Independence Day address from the Red Fort, warning that while armed Naxals were gone from the forests, intellectual Naxals were lurking in the corridors of power and pushing for anarchy. Modi reiterated the attack on Saturday during the centenary celebrations of Delhi University’s Shri Ram College of Commerce, calling the remark a homoeopathic pill that caused mental Naxals to come out. He also introduced a new phrase, naraz fufa, to describe critics who question national projects like statues, bullet trains, and the new Parliament. The remarks drew sharp condemnation from opposition leaders. Senior Congress leader P Chidambaram and Aam Aadmi Party national convenor Arvind Kejriwal both stated they were proud to be dimagi Naxals. Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge accused Modi of using adjectives like naraaz phoopa, dimagi Naxal, urban Naxal, Khan Market gang, andolan-jeevi, and parjeevi as a modus operandi to suppress democratic questions and hide government failures. Union Minister Kiren Rijiju defended the Prime Minister on X, clarifying that the remark did not target opposition leaders, but rather those who support Maoists, reject the Indian Constitution, stand with separatists, or support Article 370.
