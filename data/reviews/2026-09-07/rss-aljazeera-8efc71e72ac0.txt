═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The first road bridge connecting Russia and North Korea opened on Monday as flag-adorned trucks crossed the one-kilometer, two-lane structure spanning the Tumen River. Named after Red Army soldier Yakov Novichenko, the bridge was agreed upon during Russian President Vladimir Putin's visit to North Korea in 2024, with construction beginning in April of that year. Russian Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin and North Korea Premier Pak Thae-song addressed workers and officials via video link for the opening. Mishustin stated that up to 300 vehicles will be able to cross every day and that the bridge connects to Russia's federal road network. Cargo transport began immediately, and the bridge is scheduled to be fully operational for passenger traffic by the end of the year. The two countries were previously connected only by air and rail routes, alongside the existing rail link known as the Friendship Bridge commissioned in 1959. Mishustin said the expansion of cross-border transport infrastructure will provide a powerful impetus for further development, calling the opening a genuinely historic event and a new symbol of friendship. Pak Thae-song stated the bridge will cement cooperation to fulfill the hopes and aspirations of both peoples. The project marks a milestone in relations that have grown considerably stronger and accelerated since Russia launched its war on Ukraine in February 2022, a conflict where North Korea sent thousands of troops to Russia's Kursk region.
