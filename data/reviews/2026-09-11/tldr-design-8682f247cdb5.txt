═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI Group PBC launched ChatGPT Images 2.5 today, an update to version 2.0 that debuted in April. The new tool generates images with more natural lighting, richer textures, and up to 50 percent faster speeds while improving multi-turn instruction following. A major addition is Sketch, a feature allowing users to draw on touchscreens with a finger or stylus to serve as a visual reference. Version 2.5 also better preserves subject details, facial features, and unmodified elements across multiple edits and long conversations. The update introduces new templates for formats like flyers and product photos, supports inline comments for targeted edits, and lets users share prompts with generated images. OpenAI offers two variants of the model. GPT-Image-2.5 Flare serves as the default, producing high-quality images two to four times faster than the GPT-Image-2 model with support for transparent backgrounds. GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst trades speed for greater precision and fidelity, targeting product photos and marketing materials requiring pixel-perfect consistency. For security, the model includes prompt and output guardrails alongside C2PA metadata and invisible watermarking to prevent deepfakes and unauthorized reuse. The new model is available now in ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex across desktop, mobile, and web platforms, with both variants also accessible to developers via separate application programming interfaces.
