═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Apple unveiled its first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, starting at $1,999, during its annual fall event at its headquarters in Cupertino, California. Newly minted CEO John Ternus, who took over from Tim Cook on September 1, hosted the keynote. The Duo features a passport-like book design with a 5.4-inch front display and a 7.6-inch interior display. Apple also introduced the iPhone 18 Pro at $1,199 and the iPhone 18 Pro Max at $1,299, both featuring faster A20 Pro processors, improved batteries delivering 24 and 30 hours of charge respectively, and a variable mechanical aperture. Additionally, the company debuted the Apple Watch Series 12, Ultra 4, and AirPods 5, with the new AirPods priced at $129. IDC analyst Francisco Jeronimo noted the strategic significance of the launches, stating, Each new product adds meaningful improvements, but the iPhone Duo is the event's strategic flagship and a direct test of whether Apple can use design, ecosystem integration, and premium positioning to reshape a segment that has so far remained selective.
