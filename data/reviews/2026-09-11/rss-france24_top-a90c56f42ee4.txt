═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

During his visit to Paris for the International Space Summit hosted by the French government on September 9 and 10, Luxembourg Prime Minister Luc Frieden addressed rising far-right political breakthroughs in Europe, including the AfD’s victory in the Saxony-Anhalt regional election at the beginning of September. Frieden stated that while he strongly supports democratic elections, the rise of extremists is concerning because nationalism and the discrimination of minorities led to the Second World War. As he put it, of course we live in democracies, so it is for the people of the respective EU countries to vote for their leaders, and I am a very strong supporter of democratic elections, yet at the same time, people should realise what would happen if we didn’t have Europe. Frieden added that political centrists must prove they can deliver on the promise of peace and economic prosperity. 

Addressing European space policy and the notable absence of major American aerospace companies from the summit amid an ongoing feud between Brussels and the Trump administration, Frieden emphasized that strategic autonomy means the freedom to decide for yourself, but does not mean that you exclude all the others. He noted that Europe has strong companies building rockets and satellites, alongside a leading role in quantum technology for intercontinental satellite communication, while affirming that America remains a friend whose technology is still needed. Regarding the EU being sidelined from US diplomatic efforts over Ukraine in early September, Frieden stated that Europe cannot act as a mediator because it is clearly on the side of Ukraine, supporting the fundamental principle that borders cannot be changed by force. He concluded that Europe supports US efforts to find a solution while working to ensure the European voice is heard and economic links with America are preserved.
