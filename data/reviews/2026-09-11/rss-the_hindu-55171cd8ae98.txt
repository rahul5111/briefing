═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

With an eye on the 2027 Assembly poll, the Punjab BJP announced on Friday, September 11, 2026, that it will launch ‘Nasha Mukt Punjab Yatras’ from September 18 to highlight the drug menace in the State. State BJP president Kewal Singh Dhillon, addressing a press conference alongside party national vice-president Manpreet Badal and former State chief Sunil Jakhar, stated that the yatras will cover all 117 Assembly constituencies to turn the fight against drugs into a mass movement. The campaign follows a row over the alleged suicide of a Dalit villager who was reportedly pressured to apologise for questioning Finance Minister Harpal Singh Cheema regarding the supply of synthetic drugs known as chitta in the State. Mr. Dhillon identified the growing drug menace, deteriorating law and order, and corruption as the three major challenges confronting Punjab. Describing the campaign's broader impact, Mr. Dhillon said, “The yatras are not merely an anti-drug awareness campaign, but an attempt to confront the grave threat that it poses to Punjabiyat, our culture, values and the proud identity of the land of the gurus.” He added that the crisis is tearing families apart, pushing youth toward violence, and imposing a heavy financial burden that forces families into debt or to sell land and assets for treatment. BJP president Nitin Nabin will flag off the first yatra on September 18 from Pathankot district. Subsequent marches will begin in Fatehgarh Sahib on September 19, Fazilka on September 20, and Talwandi Sabo on September 21. In March, Union Home Minister Amit Shah promised that the BJP would free the State from drugs within two years if given a chance to form the next government.
