═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

US President Donald Trump stated on Wednesday at a Republican midterm convention in Dallas, Texas, that the war with Iran will end immediately after the November 7 midterm elections. Trump defended the conflict as a measure to prevent Tehran from developing a nuclear weapon and promised that oil prices would fall upon winning the war. On Wednesday, Brent crude rose to over $100 per barrel following recent fighting that included US strikes on Iranian oil tankers in retaliation for targeting a US warship. 

Middle East expert Mohammad Ghaedi noted that Iran views escalation as a controlled instrument of coercive bargaining rather than an end in itself, stating that Iran is trying to impose costs on the United States without raising them so dramatically that Washington feels compelled to return to full-scale war. Tehran's preferred outcome is a return to the June Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding, though Ghaedi noted its first provision requiring an Israeli withdrawal from Lebanon makes this unlikely before Israel's October 27 parliamentary election. 

Senior advisers reportedly urged Trump on September 2 to limit escalation to avoid political repercussions for Republicans, while the White House considers expanding military action after the elections. Economically, professor Ahmad Alavi warned that pressure alone will not yield political concessions and could instead trigger military escalation if Iran views the pressure as a threat to its survival. Meanwhile, Gulf state foreign ministers plan to meet their Iranian counterpart in Salalah, Oman, next week to pursue a diplomatic solution for commercial shipping safety through the Strait of Hormuz. 

Publicly, Iranian leaders maintain a hard line. Deputy parliamentary speaker Ali Nikzad stated on September 9 that the war will continue until final victory, and Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps spokesman Hossein Mohebbi demanded on September 8 that the US completely cease hostilities. On September 10, the IRGC released a video showing the reported capture of a US Anduril Dive-LD unmanned underwater vehicle in the Strait of Hormuz.
