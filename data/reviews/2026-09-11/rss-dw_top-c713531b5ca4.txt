A train carrying 180 passengers derailed on Friday evening on the route between the cities of Rouen and Caen in Normandy, northwest France. The accident occurred near the town of Cleon at around 7:54 p.m. local time, or 17:54 UTC. Local authorities reported that at least 44 people were injured. Jean-Benoit Albertini, head of the local authority, stated that one of the injured was evacuated by helicopter in a critical state. French Transport Minister Philippe Tabarot said 140 firefighters were deployed to the scene. A police spokesperson stated the train derailed after hitting an unidentified object, and the cause of the accident remains under investigation. French rail network operator SNCF Reseau suspended traffic on the line, stating, "The priority is to care for the passengers and then to restore safe operating conditions." Local authorities urged the public to avoid the area.
