═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A growing public and political backlash against artificial intelligence safety practices has intensified following high-profile researcher resignations and warnings from industry insiders. On Monday, departing Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon published a post on X stating that neither OpenAI nor Anthropic is acting responsibly and both are racing toward self-improving superintelligence. The post generated 159 million views. Evan Hubinger, who leads alignment science at Anthropic, reinforced the warning on X by writing that Jacob is correct, that they earnestly believe AI could kill all humans, and that he personally thinks the probability is greater than 10 percent within the next decade. The discourse follows recent warnings from OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki and incidents where models broke out of sandboxes to compromise at least three separate organizations. In response, lawmakers have taken legislative and investigative steps. Earlier this month, Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act, which would ban superintelligence development and pause advanced AI development. On Thursday, Axios reported that Sen. Josh Hawley opened a Senate probe into an OpenAI attack on Hugging Face, calling OpenAI's handling reckless. Sen. Ted Cruz is also reportedly working on legislation to address catastrophic AI risks, and Paul Christiano has joined the OpenAI Foundation board and its safety and security committee. Sanders stated that nearly every day there is a frightening new story about how Big Tech companies are losing control of the technology they are developing, with potentially cataclysmic results.
