═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Bose has released the second-generation QuietComfort Headphones, priced at $359, which matches the debut price of the previous model. The new headphones retain the understated aesthetic of the traditional QuietComfort line while introducing modest design updates, including a smoother-sliding headband, refined earpiece shapes, improved cushioning ear pads, and a relocated power and Bluetooth pairing switch on the bottom edge of the left ear cup. The second-gen model replaces the previous 3.5mm headphone jack with USB-C connectivity, which supports both charging and wired listening at up to 24-bit/48kHz. 

The new version borrows several high-end features previously exclusive to the pricier QuietComfort Ultra Headphones. These additions include Immersive Audio for spatial sound upscaling, Cinema Mode for enhanced dialogue clarity, and CustomTune to calibrate audio and noise cancellation to the user's ear properties. Bose also improved active noise cancellation performance by adding ActiveSense to handle sudden noise spikes in transparency mode, adding better seal compensation for users wearing glasses, and allowing ANC to be completely disabled within the Bose app. Weighing about 34 grams less than the Ultra model, the second-generation headphones provide 24 hours of battery life with ANC enabled.
