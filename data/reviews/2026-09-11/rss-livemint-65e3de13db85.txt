═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Former Fox News anchor Maria Bartiromo has broken her silence one week after the network announced they were parting ways, using social media to thank supporters and hint at her future. Bartiromo posted a message on X on Thursday, September 10, the day before her 59th birthday, accompanied by a photograph of a rainbow breaking through clouds. She wrote, Thank you to all friends and followers for your amazing words I am so grateful for the outpouring of love on SM and texts. I love you all. here is a great shot of the breaking through, I snapped the other day. More to come. The post did not address the circumstances of her exit, though her attorney, Bryan Freedman, previously disputed reports that she was fired, calling such claims absolutely and unequivocally false. Fox News Media announced on September 3 that Bartiromo was no longer with the company, ending a 12 and a half year tenure. The network stated the decision was effective immediately and thanked her for her work, offering no further explanation. The separation prompted immediate programming changes, including the renaming of her weekday show Mornings with Maria to Mornings with FOX Business with rotating anchors, the rebranding of Maria Bartiromo's Wall Street to FBN's Wall Street, and temporarily handing Sunday Morning Futures to Jason Chaffetz. Bartiromo joined Fox in 2014 following a twenty-year career at CNBC. Following the announcement, Donald Trump praised Bartiromo as a true warrior.
