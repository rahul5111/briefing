═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Apple has officially announced the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max during its event today, carrying over the design of the previous generation while introducing new back designs and four color options consisting of black, glacier blue, silver, and burgundy red. 

Both models feature a smaller Dynamic Island capable of displaying three Live Activities simultaneously and run on the new A20 Pro chip. Built on a 2 nanometer process, the chip includes a 6 core CPU with 2 super cores and 4 efficiency cores, a 7 core GPU delivering 40 percent faster graphics performance, a 32 core Neural Engine alongside a second Neural Engine, and a 50 percent increase in memory bandwidth. The A20 Pro is directly connected to a next generation vapor chamber for improved thermal management and sustained performance. 

The devices boast the longest battery life in an iPhone, with the iPhone 18 Pro matching the previous iPhone 17 Pro Max battery life, and the iPhone 18 Pro Max delivering up to 30 hours of battery life alongside faster wired charging. Camera updates include a 48 megapixel Fusion main camera featuring variable aperture on both models, faster Night mode captures, manual control options for aperture and shutter speed, a smart focus mode, advanced photographic styles, and an Apple Reference Image feature that stores metadata and a reference image to verify a photo is not AI generated. 

The iPhone 18 Pro starts at 1,199 dollars and introduces a 2 terabyte storage option for the first time, while the iPhone 18 Pro Max starts at 1,299 dollars, marking a 100 dollar price increase over the 17 Pro models. Apple is also releasing new accessories, including an updated Clear Case design, a wrist strap, and new colors of silicone and FineWoven cases, alongside the U.S. launch of the AppleCare One Family plan. Pre-orders begin on September 12, with the official launch scheduled for September 18.
