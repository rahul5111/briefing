═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. reported record monthly revenue for August on Thursday, driven by strong demand for artificial intelligence chips. The world's largest contract chipmaker posted revenue of $514.8 billion New Taiwan dollars, equivalent to $16.35 billion, marking a 53.3% increase from a year earlier and a 10.1% jump from July. This follows a second-quarter profit increase of more than 77% and marks the fourth consecutive month of rising monthly revenue. TrendForce data released Wednesday showed TSMC maintained a 72.5% market share in the global foundry market during the second quarter, while Samsung Foundry ranked second with 5.9% and China's SMIC followed with 5.4%. Strong demand for AI server processors kept TSMC's advanced 5-, 4-, and 3-nanometer capacity fully booked through the quarter. Separately, TSMC and ASML announced an initiative to advance next-generation chipmaking technology, with TSMC planning to use ASML's High NA technology in large-scale manufacturing starting in 2030 as AI applications increasingly demand more complex transistor architectures.
