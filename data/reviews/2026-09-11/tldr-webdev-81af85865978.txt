═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Shopify is transitioning its mobile applications back to native Swift and Kotlin, moving away from React Native, which it adopted in 2020. The decision follows a reevaluation prompted by the growing capabilities of coding models and AI agents, which have reduced the historical cost and maintenance overhead of building and maintaining features across two separate platforms. 

To execute the migration without compromising quality, Shopify built an internal system called Helix that processes code in small, reviewed checkpoints requiring tests and human approval. The Shop app was fully rebuilt and published in app stores in 12 weeks, while the migration of the primary Shopify app is underway to ship later this year. Shopify's other mobile apps, including Point of Sale and Inbox, will follow. 

Alongside the migration, Shopify announced changes to its open-source libraries. The company will sponsor FlashList through the end of 2026, with William Candillon planning to fork the repository to publish it under a new name, while Shopify remains in discussions with other companies regarding long-term stewardship. The Restyle repository will be archived and kept working through the end of 2026 before maintenance stops.
