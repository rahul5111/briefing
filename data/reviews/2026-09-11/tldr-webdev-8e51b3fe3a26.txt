═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A new vulnerability dubbed the deathray allows an untrusted website to completely freeze a Mac's graphics and desktop UI simply by having a user click a link. Discovered by Auberon López on September tenth, two thousand twenty-six, the exploit reproduces cross-browser on macOS across Chrome, Firefox, and Safari on M-series MacBooks running Tahoe, though Intel Macs and Max M-series models appear less susceptible. 

The attack utilizes WebGPU shaders to execute an infinite busy loop in a compute shader while a vertex shader attempts to read from the same data buffer. This creates a resource pileup that overwhelms the macOS WindowServer process, rendering the UI unresponsive and eventually triggering a kernel panic and system restart via the watchdog timer. 

Apple previously addressed a similar WebGL-based exploit in 2023 called ShadyShader, which resulted in CVE-2023-40441 with a six point five medium CVSS score. López disclosed the deathray vulnerability to Apple Security on July twenty-seventh, two thousand twenty-six. While Apple initially intended to fix the issue, the company stated on August twenty-sixth that it did not see security implications in the report, categorizing the outcome as a hang or crash rather than a security issue, and declined to issue a product change.
