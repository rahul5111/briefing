Shopify has acquired the Tailwind CSS framework, launched over nine years ago. The framework is currently installed more than 110 million times per week and is used by companies including ChatGPT, X, Cloudflare, Reddit, and Shopify. According to the announcement, nothing will change regarding Tailwind CSS or other open-source projects, which will remain MIT-licensed and led by the existing team with Shopify's support. On the commercial side, sign-ups for new customers are closing, while all existing customers will maintain access to products like Tailwind Plus and ui.sh. The acquisition provides Tailwind a stable long-term home where it can be actively maintained and developed in service of a real product at Shopify, addressing the needs of merchants, custom storefront design, admin management, and checkout experiences.
