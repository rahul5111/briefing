═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The new documentary short Love Rendered explores memory loss and technology by following Burt and Ethelle Shatz, a couple married for over seventy years, as they navigate Burt's cognitive decline. The film focuses on recreating an unrecorded memory from the day they met at a student co-op in Cleveland, which previously existed only in their minds. The documentary was directed by Academy Award-nominated filmmaker Liz Garbus and produced alongside Dan Cogan and Darren Aronofsky, in a collaboration between Google DeepMind and Aronofsky's creative venture, Primordial Soup. To reconstruct the unphotographed day, Ethelle worked with the technical team as an active co-creator, correcting details like the curve of a staircase or the shape of a shoe heel. Engineers used generative models to restore black-and-white photos of the couple from their youth, and performance capture models to map Burt and Ethelle's present-day micro-mannerisms, such as the specific tilt of Burt's head and the subtle crinkle around his eyes, onto their younger likenesses. Reflecting on the collaborative process, Darren Aronofsky noted during production that a tool does nothing until it is guided by human hands.
