═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Madurai Bench of the Madras High Court praised the Madurai district administration for its arrangements ahead of the Sri Meenakshi Sundareswarar temple consecration ceremony. On September 11, 2026, a Division Bench of Justices C.V. Karthikeyan and R. Sakthivel dismissed petitions filed by G. Theeran Thirumurugan, M.V. Rameshkumar, and G.D. Manikandan that had sought court directions for smooth conduct of the event. The court noted that the Madurai District Collector had issued necessary instructions to authorities, the Madurai Corporation had taken steps to clean the precincts, and temple authorities had ensured no obstructions for the anticipated large crowds of devotees. Declaring that no court directions were required, the bench stated, "We are confident that the temple administration will follow the long standing and centuries old practice in the Kumbhabhishekam. It would only be prudent on our part to leave the matter to the district administration to take care of steps to be taken for the Kumbhabhishekam. No directions are required."
