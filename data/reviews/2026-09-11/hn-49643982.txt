═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Shopify is moving its mobile tech stack from React Native back to native Swift and Kotlin, citing advancements in coding models and AI agents that have removed the historic cost of building and maintaining features twice. The company originally adopted React Native in 2020 to streamline development, but found that modern LLMs now allow agents to handle implementation, translation, testing, and review work across platforms. 

To manage the migration, Shopify built a system called Helix that breaks work into small, reviewed checkpoints requiring tests and human sign-off. It also developed a CLI-based architecture that decouples business logic from the UI, letting agents test and iterate in milliseconds instead of using slow mobile simulators. 

The Shop app was fully rebuilt and published in the app stores using this AI-assisted approach in 12 weeks. Migration of the larger Shopify app, which includes over 300 screens, is underway and scheduled to ship later this year, followed by remaining apps like Point of Sale and Inbox. 

As part of the transition, Shopify will continue sponsoring its open-source library FlashList—which draws roughly two million downloads a week—through the end of 2026, while discussing long-term stewardship with other companies and planning for a fork managed by William Candillon. The Restyle library will also be supported through the end of 2026 before its repository is archived.
