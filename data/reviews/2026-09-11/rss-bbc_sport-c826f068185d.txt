Hull City have been fined £30,000 by the Football Association following homophobic chanting during their 4-0 FA Cup defeat by Chelsea in February. The club accepted the charge after spectators repeatedly ignored PA system warnings and messages displayed on screens during the match at the MKM Stadium. 

Following the incident, three supporters have been banned for a minimum of three years, and three people were arrested for allegedly engaging in the chanting. Fan groups condemned the behavior as a stain on the game. 

Ahead of their Premier League match against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on Saturday, Hull City is urging supporters not to repeat the chant. The club stated that discrimination of any kind is wholly unacceptable and will not be tolerated, warning that anyone using the homophobic slur faces arrest, criminal charges, and stadium bans. In a statement, Hull City said, "Hull City condemns, in the strongest possible terms, the offensive and inappropriate language used by the supporters responsible, and its use may constitute a hate crime."
