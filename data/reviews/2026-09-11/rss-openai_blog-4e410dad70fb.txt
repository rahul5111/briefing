═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI is launching the GPT-Live-1 model in the API at a cost of $0.05 per minute for the front-end voice layer, enabling developers to build natural voice-enabled applications and business workflows. First introduced in ChatGPT, the model listens and speaking simultaneously, reasoning over incoming and outgoing audio together in a single architecture to avoid the latency of chained speech-to-text, reasoning, and text-to-speech systems. 

Key capabilities include smooth interruption handling, tool calling delegation to backend models like GPT-6 Astra, tone and pace customization via system prompts, silent context management, background noise handling, long-session reliability, and full-duplex telephony support. In early evaluations, language learning platform Speak found that GPT-Live-1 cut interruptions by almost 80% compared to previous turn-based systems by giving learners more time to think before responding. 

The model natively provides automatic speech recognition transcripts, response text, alphanumeric understanding, keyword biasing, and turn detection support. In company evaluations, GPT-Live-1 improves Full Duplex Bench performance by 30 percentage points over GPT-Realtime-2.1. When paired with GPT-6 Astra at medium reasoning effort, it ranks first on Tau3, which measures end-to-end voice-agent intelligence. Additionally, OpenAI is expanding its voice selection to include a broader range of accents, dialects, and languages. Enterprises can also build voice workflows using OpenAI Presence, which uses GPT-Live-1 to power real-time interactions for tasks such as customer support, system navigation, and human escalation.
