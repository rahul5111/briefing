═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A volcanic eruption from Anak Krakatau in Indonesia has left more than 150,000 passengers stranded after authorities temporarily suspended operations at eight airports. The closures began Sunday morning with Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta International Airport after volcanic ash was detected in the airspace. Transport minister Dudy Purwagandhi stated that 712 flights have been affected, with the pause impacting routes to Singapore, Doha, Sydney, and Kuala Lumpur. Tests confirmed the ash spread toward West Java, reaching 20,000 feet east of the volcano and 50,000 feet west toward Sumatra, according to the meteorological agency BMKG. The volcano began erupting late Friday night with continuous seismic activity and lava fountains, followed by two more eruptions on Sunday morning. Authorities confirmed no tsunami warning is linked to the activity. The health minister urged residents to stay indoors and wear face masks, while local schools cancelled activities after students and residents reported eye irritation. Resident Aldi Candra Prayogi described the conditions to local newspaper Kompas, saying it felt like small grains of sand had gotten into his eyes and noting that the dust is so thick he has to sweep his front yard every five minutes. Minister Purwagandhi asked airlines to consider flight diversions, expedite ticket refunds, and provide timely information to prevent airport overcrowding.
