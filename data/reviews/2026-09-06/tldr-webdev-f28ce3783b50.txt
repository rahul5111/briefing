In the Coding Agent Index, GPT-6 Astra scores 67 on Codex, placing it roughly even with Claude Opus 5, Fable 5, and Muse Spark 1.3, while trailing the leader Fable 5.1 at 70. The model achieves strong token efficiency in coding tasks, using one-third of the tokens of GPT-5.6 Sol at max effort and one-fifth of Claude Opus 5, allowing its max effort setting to match GPT-5.6 Sol's cost while scoring two points higher.

In the Intelligence Index, GPT-6 Astra scores 61, matching GPT-5.6 Sol but trailing Claude Fable 5.1 by 5 points and Meta's Muse Spark 1.3. Although it uses about 10% fewer output tokens at max effort than its predecessor, the 2.5x price hike makes it 75% more expensive per task. 

Performance across other evaluations is mixed.
