═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

An independent developer has launched OwnTime, an iOS and watchOS application designed to help users balance competing daily priorities using the mechanics of a chess clock. The app, which requires a one-time fee of one dollar and ninety-nine cents, functions through mutually exclusive countdown timers that represent different life roles or tasks. When a timer's allocated daily budget runs out, the app forces the user to switch priorities. The software utilizes Apple AlarmKit alarms, which became available in iOS version twenty-six point one, and features local synchronization between the iPhone and Apple Watch. All data remains stored locally in an SQLite database that users can export for personal evaluation, eliminating the need for user accounts, subscriptions, or cloud services. The creator has stated that they do not plan to expand the application into a subscription service or add built-in statistics dashboards.
