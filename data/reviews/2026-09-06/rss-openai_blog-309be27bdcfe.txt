═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The AI model Astra has become the first to meet the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold under the organization Preparedness Framework. Evaluations show that Astra can autonomously find previously unknown security flaws and develop exploit chains across hardened systems, scoring 100% on ExploitBench and outperforming GPT-5.6 Sol on internal benchmarks by discovering two zero-day vulnerabilities during testing. 

Development and release parts were previously delayed following an incident, leading to strengthened infrastructure, isolation controls, and a paused large frontier reinforcement learning run that restarted on August 28th. To mitigate risks from malicious actors and unauthorized actions, production safeguards include a cyber jailbreak refusal rate of 91.5% compared to 59% for GPT-5.6 Sol, conservative boundaries for high-risk accounts, chain-of-thought monitoring, and automatic containment mechanisms. 

Astra will be released soon with limited access. Advanced cybersecurity capabilities will initially be restricted to a small group of alpha testers before expanding to defensive use through Daybreak Blue. Further details regarding safety evaluations will be provided in the model system card at launch.
