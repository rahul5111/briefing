═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

An Amazon Prime Air cargo plane arriving from San Juan, Puerto Rico, overran a runway at Miami International Airport on Sunday afternoon at about 2 p.m., striking multiple vehicles and bursting into flames. The incident prompted a major emergency response, the closure of all runways and taxiways at the airport, and a full ground stop issued by the Federal Aviation Administration. More than 60 Miami-Dade Fire Rescue units responded to the scene to extinguish the fire and assess those affected, though authorities did not immediately release information regarding injuries or fatalities. Images from the crash site showed the aircraft resting on its belly beside a roadway near two semi-trucks, remaining largely intact despite intense fire and thick black smoke. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy stated that the FAA was dispatching investigators to examine the incident, and the National Transportation Safety Board announced it was gathering information to assist in determining the cause. The runway closures disrupted air traffic at the major South Florida hub, with the FAA warning that delays would continue even after reopening, as arrivals and departures were expected to operate at reduced capacity. Amazon did not immediately respond to requests for comment as authorities continued emergency operations and investigated the crash.
