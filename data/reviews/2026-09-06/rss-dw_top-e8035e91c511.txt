═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner arrived in Kyiv by train from Poland on Sunday for their first official visit to Ukraine, following more than three hours of talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow a day earlier. Putin had declared a three-day ceasefire to ensure the envoys safety. The Moscow meeting produced no immediate announcements, though Putin’s foreign policy adviser Yuri Ushakov described the talks as constructive, frank, and useful, noting they also focused on economic issues and potential large Russia-US projects. Ahead of the visit, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy wrote on social media that Kyiv is ready for a constructive discussion, stating that peace, security guarantees, and a just and sustainable postwar peace are needed, and that Ukrainian proposals are prepared. According to Reuters, national security advisors from the United Kingdom, France, and Germany were also in Kyiv for talks with the US envoys. The diplomatic push comes as efforts by US President Donald Trump to end the four-and-a-half-year-old conflict stalled following inconclusive talks at the Kremlin in January. Negotiators have previously struggled to bridge gaps on key issues, including the future of the Donbas region. Meanwhile, Zelenskyy chief of staff Kyrylo Budanov told the New York Times last weekend that he expects the fighting to continue for many months and does not anticipate an end to the war before spring 2027, alongside predicting further Russian strategic air strikes this winter.
