═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google is launching the Fairwind Program to provide a trusted group of government agencies, enterprise customers, and cybersecurity partners with access to advanced AI tools designed to autonomously find and fix software vulnerabilities. The initiative combines Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber model with the CodeMender harness, allowing defenders to generate verified, deployment-ready code patches in minutes within a secure cloud environment at a fraction of the operating cost of traditional frontier models. Initial access is being staged for more than 650 participating global partners across government and national cyber authorities, critical infrastructure operators in sectors such as healthcare, telecommunications, energy, and finance, and core technology platforms. Participating organizations must adhere to strict operational standards, including limiting access to internal cybersecurity, incident response, or penetration testing teams and implementing protections like multi-factor authentication. Alongside the program launch, Google released its 2026 Google.org US Cybersecurity Impact Report, detailing $36 million in funding distributed across 35 cyber clinics that provide free security support to over 1,250 U.S. hospitals, public school districts, and municipal utilities, bringing Google's total global cybersecurity funding through Google.org to more than $100 million.
