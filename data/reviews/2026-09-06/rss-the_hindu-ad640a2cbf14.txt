═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Krishna Bhardwaj, a DUSU election campaigner from Gwalior, was among the first responders who pulled people from the rubble following a five-storey building collapse in Satya Niketan on Sunday, September 6, 2026. Bhardwaj and a team of 25 to 30 members arrived before police and fire personnel, rescuing four people. During the operation, Bhardwaj sustained a foot injury from a falling stone while removing a body and was taken to the hospital for treatment. He noted that seven students had been shifted to the hospital while casualties were still being assessed, and alleged that many area buildings are unauthorised and operate on fake NOCs. 

Eyewitnesses reported that the building had been closed for two years before reopening about a year prior, and that basement construction had been underway for the past two months. Dal Bahadur, a local music shop owner, stated he saw trucks carrying away mud and stones excavated from the basement over recent weeks. Dhruv Singh Bhatia, a third-year Motilal Nehru College student, had visited the building two days prior to check on first-year students. He stated that 30 to 35 students lived in the cramped structure, with three to four students crammed into single room-kitchen sets, and noted damp walls, a narrow entry, and ongoing basement excavation for commercial purposes accompanied by accumulated water.
