═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

ATV Big Air Tour co-founders Larissa Guetter and Derek Guetter use ChatGPT Work to manage administrative tasks for their live-action stunt show, which runs approximately 26 tour dates across the United States from May to November. Operating as a two-person team, they adopted ChatGPT Work to handle workloads across marketing and merchandising. The tool reduced event listing review time from about eight hours to one hour per week by automating the manual fact-checking of roughly 30 online publications daily. For merchandise management, uploading photos of items to ChatGPT Work allowed the system to organize inventory, generate a spreadsheet, build a visual inventory website, and produce reorder recommendations in under 15 minutes. The full inventorying and reordering process dropped from two to three full days to two to three hours. To improve discoverability, Larissa set up a daily ChatGPT Work automation focused on answer engine optimization to audit the company website for structured data and ticket details. Website analytics measured OpenAI search and user-bot hits rising from 183 to 2,421 across consecutive 30-day periods, marking a 1,223 percent month-over-month increase after excluding training bots and other platforms. As Larissa stated about operating the business with the tool, we are a team of two, but using ChatGPT Work lets us compete with businesses that have much bigger budgets and teams, helping us do the work of multiple team members.
