Reigning Premier League champions Arsenal secured a 2-1 comeback victory against Chelsea in a derby on Sunday, maintaining a maximum nine points from their opening three games of the campaign. Chelsea took an early lead in the second minute through £117m signing Morgan Rogers, but Arsenal responded with goals from Kai Havertz before half-time and captain Martin Odegaard in the 50th minute. 

During the summer transfer window, Arsenal spent close to £200m without securing an elite forward, missing out on targets including Julian Alvarez, Morgan Rogers, Bradley Barcola, and Vinicius Jr. Meanwhile, opponents Chelsea have spent close to £350m since the end of last season. Despite their rivals' heavy spending, Mikel Arteta's side have now won eight league games in a row stretching back to last season and conceded only one goal in their first three fixtures. 

Our mentality is to keep fighting. We have shown that many times, said Odegaard. We never want to concede early, but the reaction is top. We got our reward in the end and the response was brilliant. 

Odegaard has scored three goals in four appearances across all competitions this season, a sharp increase from his single goal in 36 matches during the previous term. Arsenal's strong start mirrors their historical campaigns in 1924-25 and 2003-04, the only other times they won their opening three top-flight games while conceding fewer than two goals. 

Former Premier League winner Chris Sutton told BBC Sport that he cannot see anyone stopping Arsenal, describing them as a powerhouse. Arsenal will now travel to Italy to face Napoli on Wednesday for the launch of their Champions League campaign, followed by a Premier League match at Sunderland on Saturday.
