═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Seattle Times and Newsday have filed a copyright infringement lawsuit against OpenAI and Microsoft over the alleged use of their journalism to train artificial intelligence models. The lawsuit, filed in response to generative AI tools like ChatGPT and CoPilot, argues that the technology threatens to leave the journalism industry broken beyond repair. The complaint describes the AI systems as rapacious consumers that devour human authored content to deliver copies and derivative imitations for commercial objectives. This legal action follows a similar 2023 lawsuit by The New York Times against the same defendants, joined by other publications since. The Seattle Times action stands out because Microsoft and OpenAI have previously provided funding for some of the publication's journalism projects and fellowships. A Microsoft spokesperson stated that the company is surprised by the lawsuit but remains willing to sit down and explore solutions to the dispute.
