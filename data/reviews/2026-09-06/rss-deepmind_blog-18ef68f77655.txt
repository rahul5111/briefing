═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Google has announced the release of Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, marking its third Flash release in six weeks. Gemini 3.8 Flash is priced at seventy-five cents per million input tokens and three dollars and seventy-five cents per million output tokens, matching the cost of the previous 3.7 version. It delivers improved performance in software engineering, agentic tasks, and multi-step reasoning, achieving fifty-four point nine percent on HLE-Verified. The model is available for developers through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Android Studio, and Stitch, and for enterprises via Gemini Enterprise and consumer subscriptions. The second variant, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, is a cybersecurity model focused on vulnerability detection and automated patching. It achieves a forty-seven point two percent pass rate on CWE-Bench and reaches a success rate exceeding seventy percent across complex codebases spanning twenty programming languages. Google stated that the Chrome Security team found the model produced two point six times more correct patches to vulnerabilities in Chrome than larger commercial models, and the Cloud Vulnerability Research team used it to find a critical foundational vulnerability in less than two hours. Due to its permissive mitigations for cybersecurity, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is restricted to trusted defenders through the newly launched Fairwind Program, which provides access to government authorities, critical infrastructure operators, and software maintainers.
