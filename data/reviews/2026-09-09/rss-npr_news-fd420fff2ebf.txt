═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The Transportation Security Administration is launching a new program called Gateside by TSA PreCheck that allows eligible members without plane tickets to pass through security and enter gate areas. The initiative brings back a pre-9/11 tradition that ended nearly 25 years ago. The free program is initially available at 13 airports, including Los Angeles International Airport, Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport, Harry Reid International Airport in Las Vegas, and Salt Lake City International Airport. The TSA plans to expand the program to additional airports in the coming months. Interested members must apply online one to three days before their planned visits and receive approval before heading to the airport. Approved individuals can use a TSA PreCheck lane with an acceptable form of identification. The passes are valid for one calendar day and allow for reentries on that same day. Members of other trusted-traveler programs who possess a Known Traveler Number are also eligible to apply. As the TSA stated on its website, the initiative serves as an exclusive benefit for trusted travelers to make it easier to return to the gate for welcomes and send-offs, meet friends during a layover, and enjoy airport dining and shopping.
