Mistral announced it has raised three billion euros in a Series D funding round at a post-money valuation of more than twenty-one billion euros, marking the largest equity fundraising round ever completed by a European technology company, three years after its launch. Samsung Electronics led the round, with co-leads Scaleup Europe Fund, managed by EQT, and existing investor PSG Equity. New investors joining the round include Advent, funds and accounts managed by BlackRock, and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, alongside numerous existing investors such as a16z, ASML, and NVIDIA.

The funding will expand Mistral's frontier research, scale compute capacity for training powerful models, accelerate commercial growth, and widen its international footprint. Mistral currently operates across twenty countries and supports more than one hundred twenty-five global enterprises, including Airbus, ASML, and HSBC. The company states it is the only AI company building a full stack of open-weight models, infrastructure, compute capacity, and production products to meet growing international demand for AI performance combined with data governance, independence, and control over infrastructure and the intelligence loop.
