═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Sony is not backing away from its plan to end video game discs in January 2028, but the company is not shutting down production as quickly as previously indicated. Two months after reports suggested Sony's last discmaking plant was already being repurposed, the company's discmaking division has clarified a widely reported detail. Originally, it appeared that the division's president stated the Thalgau plant would produce 90 percent fewer discs in 2028. However, an unnamed Sony DADC spokesperson told Game journalist Brian Crecente that the reality is the facility will only produce 10 percent fewer discs in 2028. In a statement to the journalist, the spokesperson said, "To clarify and avoid any misleading information, the actual figure is a 10 percent reduction by 2028 compared to our current output, rather than the 90 percent reduction that was previously communicated."
