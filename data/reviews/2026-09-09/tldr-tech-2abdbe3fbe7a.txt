═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Huawei unveiled the Mate XT 2 tri-folding phone on Monday, scheduling it to go on sale in China on September 10, just two days before Apple is expected to reveal its first foldable iPhone on September 9. The Mate XT 2 features a 10.2-inch display, a battery above 6,000mAh, a redesigned hinge to soften the screen crease, and a Kirin 9050 Pro chip built on Huawei's own newer process. Xiaomi also recently launched its 18 Fold at around 8,999 yuan, running a proprietary Xring O3 chip with LPDDR6 memory, a 7.6-inch folding display, a 200MP main camera, and 67W wired charging. These launches show Chinese manufacturers shipping flagship foldables on domestically designed silicon despite United States export controls. Apple’s expected foldable device features a 7.8-inch inner screen, a 5.3-inch cover display, no telephoto lens, Touch ID in the power button instead of Face ID, and an anticipated price above $2,000. Huawei’s distribution remains constrained by the lack of Google services, keeping its phones out of Europe and the United States in meaningful volumes and concentrating the competition with Apple inside China, where Apple's market share faces sustained pressure. Pricing outside China for the Mate XT 2 has not been published, and Apple has not yet officially announced its foldable device.
