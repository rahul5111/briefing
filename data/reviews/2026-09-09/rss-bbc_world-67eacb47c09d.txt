═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Two people were killed when Russian drones struck the Starokozache border crossing between Ukraine and Moldova, forcing the facility to close. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated that the aggression must be stopped here and now before it spreads any further. The overnight attack also injured three people, including a Moldovan driver, according to Odesa regional head Oleh Kiper and Moldova's foreign ministry. Moldovan Prime Minister Vasile Tofan noted that 17 drones struck Moldovan territory in August alone, stating that Russia started this war and alone bears responsibility. In the Ukrainian capital Kyiv, a woman was killed and 14 people, including two children, were injured after a Russian drone hit a nine-storey residential building. In Russia, officials reported that four people, including a child, were killed and 29 others injured in a Ukrainian drone attack in the Black Sea port of Novorossiysk, which governor Veniamin Kondratiev described as a massive overnight enemy drone attack. Another drone hit an industrial facility in the Arctic city of Novy Urengoy, nearly 3,000 kilometres from Ukraine, triggering a fire from falling wreckage with no injuries reported, according to regional governor Dmitry Artyukhov. Russia's defence ministry stated its forces shot down 596 Ukrainian drones overnight. The cross-border strikes occurred as Russia and Ukraine stepped up long-range attacks despite renewed diplomatic efforts, following a phone call between Russian President Vladimir Putin and US President Donald Trump about ending the war, and weekend meetings in Moscow and Kyiv involving US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff.
