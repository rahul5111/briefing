═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Security firm Calif has released a demo of WeWorm, described as the first zero-click worm to spread through WeChat calls across iOS and Android. Using AI assistance, Calif researchers found the underlying memory corruption bug in WeChat's VoIP stack and wrote a remote code execution exploit in about two days, followed by the worm in one week. The exploit allows an attacker on a victim's friend list to hijack a WeChat account while the phone is ringing, without requiring the user to answer or interact with the device. By compromising one account, the worm can automatically call and infect other contacts on both Android and iOS platforms. Calif reported the vulnerability to Tencent in July 2026, and Tencent confirmed on August 28 that the exploit had been mitigated on the server side for all users. Calif is withholding full technical details until an upcoming conference, stating the research highlights how AI is lowering the barrier for creating sophisticated cyber threats.
