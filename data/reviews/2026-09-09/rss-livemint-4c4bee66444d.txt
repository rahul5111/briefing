═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Delhi Police have arrested Sudhanshu, one of the operators of the Hostel Daze paying guest facility inside the five-storey building in Delhi's Satya Niketan area that collapsed on Sunday afternoon, September 6. The disaster resulted in seven fatalities, including five students. Sudhanshu and his business partner Shubham Tyagi had leased the property in August 2025; Shubham remains at large. Police are investigating the circumstances of the collapse and the roles of those linked to the ownership, maintenance, repair work, and PG operations. According to Deputy Commissioner of Police Amit Goel, basement repairs had been underway for eight to ten days due to waste accumulation from the rainy season, and the ground floor was being repaired and extended for commercial use. Police previously arrested property owners Hariram Bansal, 81, and his wife Urmila Gupta, 75, along with their 52-year-old son Mahesh Gupta. The older couple was placed in 14-day judicial custody while Mahesh was remanded to two days of police custody. Authorities also apprehended labor contractor Sanoj from Kasganj, Uttar Pradesh, who was allegedly supervising the basement repairs. A building contractor and a mason are also being produced in court. Following the incident, Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta ordered the Municipal Corporation of Delhi to demolish an adjacent dilapidated building for public safety. The Delhi CMO stated on X that Delhi Fire Service personnel are systematically removing students' belongings from that adjacent structure. Additionally, the Delhi government is inspecting all PG accommodations to enforce building regulations and improve safety. The Supreme Court will examine the collapse on a pan-India basis, with a bench of Justices Ahsanuddin Amanullah and R Mahadevan listing the matter for a hearing on Thursday following a mention by senior advocate and amicus curiae Ajit Sinha.
