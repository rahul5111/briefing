Code found in the latest iOS 27 beta by developer pdfu points to the introduction of four major professional photography and videography features in the stock Camera app. The discovered features include exposure scopes utilizing histograms and waveforms for accurate visual exposure, focus peaking to highlight in-focus areas with an overlay, highlight clipping indicators using zebra stripes to warn of overexposure, and true manual focus via a slider which may be limited to upcoming new iPhone models. Additional findings include an option for taking a series of self-timed photos and a new image processing approach to adapt to an expected variable aperture in the iPhone 18 Pro.
