═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

A developer has launched Show HN: Interactive Tree of Life, a new web tool created by the author of ptable.com, which has been online since 1997. The platform consolidates 11 taxonomic trees, incorporating both categorical systems like plants and animals alongside phylogenetic trees of evolutionary descent, and provides an instant crosswalk between them. Across these combined trees, the tool overlays 41 visual properties, both categorical and numerical, gathered from hundreds of biological traits and sources. Users can color the tree based on attributes such as habitat, nutrition, size, longevity, and vertical range for birds or depth for fish. The backend processes 800 gigabytes of raw data through a nine-step build process that reconciles and links the 11 trees and hundreds of traits, ensuring every node receives all properties while retaining data provenance, including original wording, citations, and licenses. The frontend utilizes a custom Miller column implementation featuring a fold-away gutter and pure DOM mutation animation without heavy frameworks.
