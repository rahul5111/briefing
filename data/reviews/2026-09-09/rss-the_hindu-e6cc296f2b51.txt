═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay will embark on his first official overseas visit on Thursday, September 10, 2026, traveling to London to attract investments and meet with entrepreneurs and industrialists. Mr. Vijay departs from Chennai airport on the night of September 10 and arrives in London on September 11, with a return to Chennai scheduled for the morning of September 17. During his week-long U.K. trip, he will highlight the state's investment potential, and a couple of important Memoranda of Understanding between the Tamil Nadu government and multinational companies are likely to be inked in his presence. This follows the recently held Vettri Tamil Nadu Investors Conclave, where MoUs were signed with 97 companies for investments worth 67,482 crore rupees. The Chief Minister will also visit motorsport facilities and a circuit in London, following his recent Assembly announcement to establish a motorsport city in Tamil Nadu, and will hold discussions with members of the Tamil diaspora. He will be accompanied by his Secretaries I and IV and senior officials of Guidance Tamil Nadu. Sources confirmed there is no change to his schedule despite reports regarding a proposed visit by Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim to Chennai. Due to the Chief Minister's absence, the launch of the TVK government’s flagship welfare scheme, Thaimaman Thanga Mothiram Thittam, which gifts a one-gram gold ring to babies born in government hospitals and was originally set for September 15 to coincide with the birth anniversary of former Chief Minister C.N. Annadurai, has been postponed. Additionally, the expansion of the Perunthalaivar Kamarajar Breakfast Scheme to students in Classes VI to VIII in government and government-aided schools, scheduled for September 17 for the birth anniversary of social reformer Periyar E.V. Ramasamy, is also likely to be postponed.
