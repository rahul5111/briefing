The emeritus role allows individuals with specialized knowledge to donate their time training and mentoring the current workforce while retaining flexibility to pursue other opportunities. Glover served as the Artemis II pilot, while Wiseman commanded the mission following 165 days aboard the International Space Station. Alongside crewmates Christina Koch and Jeremy Hansen, Glover and Wiseman flew around the Moon and splashed down on April 10, 2026, as part of the first human crew to travel to lunar orbit in more than 50 years. In their new roles, both astronauts will share their expertise with astronauts, flight controllers, and engineers as NASA prepares for the Artemis III mission in 2027 and the Artemis IV mission in 2028. NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman stated, As Reid and Victor transition to emeritus status, I’m grateful they will continue sharing what they learned with our astronauts, flight controllers, and engineers as we prepare for Artemis III in 2027 and the missions that follow.
