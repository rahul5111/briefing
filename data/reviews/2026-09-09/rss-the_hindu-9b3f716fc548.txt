═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Jana Sena Party Political Affairs Committee chairman and Civil Supplies Minister Nadendla Manohar on Wednesday directed party cadre at Tadepalli to prepare fully for upcoming local body elections in Andhra Pradesh. Addressing the concluding session of a party awareness programme, Manohar stated that party president and Deputy Chief Minister K. Pawan Kalyan will make the final decision regarding seat adjustments and allocations. 

Manohar instructed coordination committees to begin operating in active mode starting Thursday, maintain regular communication with ground-level workers, and submit reports using a special format issued by the party central office. He noted that Pawan Kalyan will be available starting September 15 for regular meetings, consultations, and teleconferences. Furthermore, Manohar stated that party representatives attending NDA parliamentary meetings must present only official party policy, leaving seat-sharing matters strictly within the party president's purview.

During the same session, Jana Sena general secretary and MSME Development Corporation chairman T. Sivashankar warned workers to remain vigilant against political opponents attempting to sow division within the party and its alliance partners. Sivashankar alleged that the YSR Congress Party may deploy money and propaganda during the elections and urged workers and Veera Mahilas to rely exclusively on information from the party central office. He cautioned the cadre against complacency resulting from being part of the government and emphasized the importance of discipline, coordination with senior leaders, and adherence to Pawan Kalyan's decisions.
