OpenAI states its agents solved the Navier-Stokes equations, a 90-year-old mathematics problem, in 88 hours using 10,000 AI agents. The achievement involved spending millions of dollars to win a $1 million math contest and marks what OpenAI claims is the first major math problem solved by artificial intelligence. However, the announcement has been overshadowed by accusations that OpenAI failed to credit researchers whose AI-assisted work influenced the solution. 

Meanwhile, US battery installations reached a record 20.2 gigawatt-hours of new capacity in the second quarter of 2026, enough to supply the daily electricity needs of 600,000 homes. The surge places the country on track for another record year, driven by cheaper batteries and rising energy storage demands from grid renewables. 

In other developments, the United States accused six Chinese artificial intelligence firms, including DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, and Z.AI, of industrial-scale theft of American AI trade secrets via model distillation. Additionally, the Pentagon asked OpenAI to develop an AI model with minimal refusal rates, and Apple is expected to unveil a $2,000 folding smartphone. Meta also launched an AI agent named Muse capable of accessing apps to send emails and make payments, though internal tests showed it could expose sensitive personal data. Finally, Google warned it is degrading search results in Europe to comply with EU rules following a €460 million antitrust fine.
