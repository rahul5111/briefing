═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

PC gaming platform GOG is tapping into nostalgia for the era of physical PC game packaging by introducing downloadable 3D models and printable, foldable high-resolution box scans for a selection of titles on its store. The newly launched Printable Big Box collection currently includes five games: Armikrog, Descent, Hitman: Codename 47, Myst, and Star Trek: 25th Anniversary. Players who own these titles on GOG will find 3D boxes listed among the included goodies before purchase. Owners can download a ZIP file containing the box art as an animated GLB object file alongside flat printable templates in PDF, SVG, and Adobe Illustrator formats. GOG describes the templates as a proper papercraft project for users who miss traditional shelves of PC game boxes. In its announcement, GOG stated, physical media doesn’t survive the way software can—boxes get lost, crushed, thrown out. Scanning them, and letting you print your own, is preservation in a different shape: the same mission that keeps these games running on modern PCs, applied to the objects they used to come in.
