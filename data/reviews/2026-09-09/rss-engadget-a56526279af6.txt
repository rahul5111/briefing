═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Apple is bringing major updates to the Home app and HomeKit with the release of iOS 27, highlighted by deep integration with Apple Intelligence. The software update introduces smarter notifications that can summarize important events, such as door movements or smoke detection, to prevent lock screen congestion for users with multiple cameras and smart doorbells. Security camera footage now supports 4K recording and utilizes AI for improved face and animal detection. Additionally, the Home app can now combine footage from multiple cameras to stitch together a sequential view of someone moving across different rooms, and generates short text descriptions of video clips alongside a new search function for past events. 

For Apple TV owners, the iOS 27 Home app adds the ability to update software across all household Apple TVs simultaneously, alongside overall improvements to app reliability. Beyond software features, leaked internal code points to upcoming hardware releases, including a new generation of the HomePod mini and a rumored device called the HomePad that combines a HomePod and an iPad into a single display featuring an Apple Watch-style Faces gallery. Apple is also reportedly developing a tabletop robot. Users who want to test the iOS 27 features early can sign up for the Apple Beta Program, though installation on a spare device is recommended to avoid potential instability.
