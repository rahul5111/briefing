═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Hurricane Lowell brought torrential rains and catastrophic winds to Hawaii’s western island of Kauai, leaving at least 30,000 residents without power. Although the Category 1 hurricane did not make direct landfall, passing about 128 kilometres west of the island, it generated wind gusts of approximately 85mph that downed trees, power poles, and lines. By early Tuesday, nearly 92 percent of Kauai Island Utility Cooperative residents were experiencing outages. 

The storm triggered coastal road flooding, washed away sections of beachside roads, and forced roughly 300 residents to evacuate. Emergency officials urged locals to stay off the roads and shelter in place while crews clear debris and assess the damage. Hawaiian mayor Derek Kawakami reported extensive damage on Tuesday, stating that everyone's participation has transitioned to gathering data and information. 

While the storm center passed west of the islands overnight and moved north into the open sea, the National Hurricane Center warned that flooding and mudslides would remain a risk. By late Tuesday morning, Lowell's maximum sustained winds decreased to near 65mph with higher gusts as it moved north at 14mph. Hurricane warnings for Kauai and Niihau, along with a tropical storm warning for Oahu, were lifted by Tuesday morning, though high surf and deadly rip currents are expected to continue affecting exposed coasts for another day or two.
