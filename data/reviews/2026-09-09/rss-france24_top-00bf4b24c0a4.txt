France's far-right National Rally party has expelled a policeman from its security team after bodycam footage published on Tuesday in the local daily Midi Libre revealed him making racist, homophobic, and sexist remarks. The footage, dated November of last year, captures the officer and colleagues in the southern town of Carcassonne using derogatory terms while on duty. 

The National Rally denounced the remarks on Wednesday as criminally punishable and entirely at odds with party values, stating it immediately removed the officer from its security detail and expelled him from the party. The Carcassonne prosecutor's office has launched an investigation into the incident.

The controversy poses a challenge to party leader Marine Le Pen ahead of next year's presidential election, as she works to soften the image of the anti-immigration party inherited from her father. Le Pen is running for president for a fourth time and currently leads polls in the first round of voting next spring. 

President Emmanuel Macron's equality minister Aurore Bergé condemned the remarks as absolutely unacceptable. Former prime minister and presidential candidate Gabriel Attal shared the video on X, writing that it depicts what a France led by the National Rally would look like.
