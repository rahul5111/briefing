═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The AI assistant Instinct, currently valued at $2.5 billion, is rolling out dedicated email addresses for all users, allowing the autonomous agent to create and run its own accounts without cluttering human inboxes. Founder Noah Shinn announced the feature on Tuesday, explaining that the email addresses will let Instinct handle tasks requiring email verification, such as signing up for services, contacting businesses, and following up on matters that need attention. As Shinn wrote on X, Instinct can use its own email to contact a restaurant about a special request, ask a business about availability, or sign up for a service it needs to complete a task. Users can also forward order confirmations to their Instinct address so the assistant can handle product returns, or add the bot to group threads to track information and answer questions about pending decisions and due dates. Earliest users already have reserved email addresses, while others can claim theirs at mail.instinct.com. This update follows a partnership with Stripe in August to enable agent purchases and a collaboration with 1Password last week to help manage user logins.
