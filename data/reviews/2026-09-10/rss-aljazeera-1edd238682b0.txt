Manchester United marked their return to the Champions League after a three-season absence with a 4-0 win against competition debutants Sabah of Azerbaijan at Old Trafford on Thursday. Despite a slow start that drew minor groans from the home crowd, United stormed into a three-goal lead by half-time. Matheus Cunha opened the scoring in the 27th minute by tapping in a low cross from Patrick Dorgu, following a nervy moment that included an early missed header by Sabah's Joy-Lance Mickels. Captain Bruno Fernandes, recently named among the candidates for the 2026 Ballon d’Or award, netted the second following a confident passing move. Benjamin Sesko added the third just before the break by rounding the goalkeeper and slotting home from a tight angle. Lisandro Martinez rounded off the victory in the second half by tapping in from a goal-line scramble after a corner. United manager Michael Carrick used all five substitutions with more than 20 minutes remaining, allowing the team to rest players ahead of this Sunday's Manchester derby. The victory, described as one of the biggest mismatches in Champions League history, does not erase underlying concerns from United's start to the domestic season, which has yielded only four points from their first three Premier League games.
