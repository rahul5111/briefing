═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Cisco Talos investigated an unusual WebDAV execution chain at a Ukrainian government organization in April 2026, where a 32-bit rundll32.exe process launched a remote DLL named verification.google. The analysis uncovered two delivery variants using disguised WebDAV DLLs and ordinal execution to deploy Amatera stealer, part of a wider credential-stealing campaign utilizing BNB Smart Chain and Cloudflare Workers for EtherHiding.

The pf.ch branch relied on a ClearFake JavaScript injection via a compromised site and Cloudflare Worker, prompting victims to execute a WebDAV path through a fake Google CAPTCHA. The resulting loader deployed Amatera build 4.1.5-alpha, which resolved its command-and-control server via a Telegra.ph page disguised as a Rust tutorial. The C2 instructed this build to deploy a NativeAOT loader running ZigCryptoStealer alongside a Go-based reverse proxy. 

Meanwhile, the verification.google branch utilized a DLL hollowing technique in dbghelp.dll and was instructed by its C2 infrastructure to install an unauthorized instance of NetSupport Manager using a Russia-based IP address. Cisco Talos tracks the threat actor behind the verification.google activity as UAT-10820 and assesses with moderate confidence that the operations are part of a broader crypto-and-credential-theft network rather than targeted attacks against specific entities.
