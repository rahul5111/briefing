═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

The v1.1 update to the reasoning-prefill experiment uses GPT-5.5 Pro as the teacher model to test open models. The evaluation consists of 45 problems, split evenly into 15 STEM, 15 non-STEM, and 15 synthetic puzzles. For each problem, the experiment generated two responses from each target model: an ordinary unprefilled response, and a response starting with the first 1% of GPT-5.5 Pro's reasoning inserted into the target model's reasoning channel, with the visible answer remaining freely generated. The author measured how much of the teacher's visible answer appeared in the first 100 tokens of the target model's answer, with each score calculated as the mean of unigram, bigram, and trigram source recall, and deltas measured in absolute percentage-point changes. Qwen, which barely moved toward Opus 4.8 in the previous experiment, shifted by plus 18.18 points toward GPT-5.5 Pro, showing a large effect on private synthetic puzzles that suggests Qwen may have learned from GPT-5.5 Pro or a closely related GPT model rather than from Opus. Kimi K3 showed the highest overlap with GPT-5.5 Pro both without and with the prefill, recording 31.11% and 35.65% respectively, though the prefill added only plus 4.54 points.
