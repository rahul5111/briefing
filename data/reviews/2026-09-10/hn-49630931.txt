═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Apple has announced the iPhone Duo, marking the company’s first foldable smartphone. When unfolded, the device features a 7.6-inch Super Retina XDR inner display that is 50 percent larger than the iPhone 18 Pro Max, alongside an outer display of matching aspect ratio. The inner screen uses a ten-layer ultrathin design with a custom nano-texture finish to reduce glare and includes a hidden under-display FaceTime camera.

Powered by the A20 Pro chip featuring a dual 16-core Neural Engine and a 7-core GPU, the iPhone Duo incorporates an internal space-saving eSIM layout and a dual-battery system to maximize capacity. Its 48MP Dual Fusion camera system includes a 48MP Fusion Main lens, a 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide lens, a 12MP Center Stage front camera, and software additions such as Smart Take, Duo Preview, and Kid Cue. 

The device supports connectivity through Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, 5G, and satellite messaging, while running on Apple Intelligence and Siri AI. Available in Star White and Night Sky color options, the iPhone Duo will be offered at apple.com, select carriers, and retail partner stores beginning on October 23, 2026. Customers can also trade in eligible devices for credit or utilize Apple Card Monthly Installments.
