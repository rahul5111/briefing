═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

US President Donald Trump announced an offer of $5,000 to every adult American if Republicans win November's midterm elections, a proposal that would cost the US government approximately $1.3 trillion for the nation's nearly 270 million adults. Speaking at a party convention in Texas on Wednesday, Trump stated the funds would have to be spent domestically, though he provided no details on how the payments would be funded or monitored, and did not specify whether he would seek legislative approval from Congress. 

Vice-President JD Vance suggested the payouts could be funded by tariff revenue and restricted from wealthy Americans. However, historical data from the Bipartisan Policy Center shows the US has brought in roughly $475 billion in tariff and excise tax revenue since the beginning of 2025, with more than $100 billion of that total subsequently refunded following a Supreme Court ruling. 

The announcement has raised legal and financial questions. Under federal law, payments intended to influence voting are illegal, but legal experts are divided. Dr Joan Mahoney at the University of Southampton told the BBC she believes the offer is legal because it would go to everyone regardless of how they voted, likening it to a tax cut pledge. Conversely, Chatham House director Laurel Rapp described it as a very different, transactional agreement. Erica York, a senior economist at the Tax Foundation, noted she would be extremely surprised if Congress approved the proposal, stating it would be one of the worst ways to use $1.3 trillion amid growing national deficits.
