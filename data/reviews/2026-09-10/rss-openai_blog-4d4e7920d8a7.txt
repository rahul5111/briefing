═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI is introducing a new Data agent in ChatGPT Work designed to allow employees to query company data, investigate changes, and build interactive dashboards using plain language without writing code or learning new analytics tools. The agent connects to approved data sources including Amazon Redshift, Datadog, Google BigQuery, ClickHouse, Databricks, MongoDB, Snowflake, Google Drive, and SharePoint. It incorporates an organization's custom calculations, business terms, and metric definitions through semantic layers and trusted sources such as Databricks Genie Ontology, dbt, GitHub, Snowflake Horizon, and BI dashboards. Enterprise administrators retain control over data connections, role-based access, and existing table, row, and column permission restrictions. The tool can also integrate and interact with platforms like Omni, Oracle BI, Power BI, Sigma, Tableau, and ThoughtSpot, and can share findings and execute approved actions through Slack or email. Organizations participating in the Alpha program include NTT Data, Thermo Fisher, and ServicePiston. OpenAI states that nearly all of its product team and over two-thirds of its go-to-market organization currently use data agents in ChatGPT Work for internal analysis. Administrators can enable and install the Data agent from the Plugins directory within ChatGPT Work workspace settings.
