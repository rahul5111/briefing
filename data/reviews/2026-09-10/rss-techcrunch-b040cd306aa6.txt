═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

OpenAI has temporarily paused new sign-ups for its two-hundred-dollar-per-month Pro subscription tier due to unprecedented infrastructure strain driven by demand for its newest and most powerful model, Astra. The decision was announced on X by Thibault Sottiaux, OpenAI product leader for core products like Codex and ChatGPT, who noted that the Pro plan places the heaviest burden on the company's systems. Sottiaux stated that the move was intended as the smallest step to maintain the broadest access possible, writing that they wanted to continue giving access while managing the load. OpenAI's other service tiers, including the API and the lower-cost Go and Plus plans, remain available to users. The company warned on Wednesday that restrictions might be necessary, with Sottiaux previously describing the demand for Astra as unprecedented and unlike anything seen during the company's past periods of steep growth. Launched on September third, Astra has been rolling out across Pro, Plus, Enterprise, and Business accounts, and was heralded by OpenAI as a generational leap and the beginning of the artificial general intelligence era. OpenAI has not disclosed how long the Pro subscriptions will remain disabled or released daily sign-up figures to quantify the scale of the demand.
