═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Seven people were injured on Thursday, September 10, 2026, when part of an under-construction four-lane bridge collapsed in Bihar’s Vaishali district. The project, located near Itwaripur village under the Lalganj police station, is part of the Union Ministry of Road Transport and Highways Bharatmala Pariyojana scheme. The incident occurred during the movement of a launching girder on the Bakarpur-Manikpur section of NH-139W due to a technical issue in the girder system. One worker remained trapped under machinery for about an hour and a half and sustained a leg fracture before being rescued with a gas cutter. All seven injured received first aid, and three were referred to Sadar Hospital in Hajipur for further treatment. Vaishali Superintendent of Police Shubhank Mishra and SDPO Gopal Mandal inspected the site, and officials have been instructed to investigate the construction company's compliance with safety standards. The National Highway Authority of India stated that a detailed technical assessment is underway to determine the cause of the malfunction. Opposition Leader Tejashwi Yadav criticized the state government over the collapse, alleging in a statement that another bridge collapsed under the weight of corruption and that substandard materials and irregularities led to the incident.
