═══ 01_DRAFT ═══

Delhi commuters face extensive travel disruptions on Friday, September 11, as traffic regulations and diversions take effect for the BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam in Pragati Maidan. The restrictions will operate from 2 pm to 8:30 pm, impacting more than 35 roads and 22 designated diversion points across central and south Delhi, the airport corridors, and railway stations. The high-profile summit runs from September 11 to 13 and brings foreign delegations and VVIP motorcades to the national capital. Nearly 4,800 traffic personnel have been deployed by Delhi Police to manage security arrangements. Non-destined commercial vehicles are barred from entering Delhi, though emergency and medical vehicles retain priority passage. Roadside parking within 200 metres of controlled corridors is prohibited. Affected roads include Sardar Patel Marg, Shanti Path, Akbar Road, Mathura Road, and Ashoka Road, while key diversion zones encompass Connaught Place, Patel Chowk, Dhaula Kuan, Moti Bagh, and Lodhi Road. Traffic may be stopped at short notice during VVIP movements, creating uncertainty and congestion on connecting corridors. The Delhi Traffic Police strongly recommends using the Metro, particularly for airport-bound passengers. Alternative routes have been established for travellers heading to IGI Airport from Gurugram, Dwarka, and various parts of the city, as well as for those commuting to New Delhi, Old Delhi, and Hazrat Nizamuddin railway stations.
